Retrieval times, and why they are printed separately

A report has two times that matter and they are not the same thing. The revision time is printed inside the PDF and tells you how old the league's information is. The retrieval time tells you how old our copy is. Both are recorded per file, so a stale publication is distinguishable from a stale source.
