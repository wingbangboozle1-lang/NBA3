Where things stand

The margin model beats a naive baseline out of sample (16.18 vs 17.30 RMSE over 114 games) but explains only 12.5% of margin variance. The totals model is worse than its baseline (20.35 vs 19.56) and is published only because the failure is disclosed. 0 of 8 candidate strategies pass all seven validation gates. The two provisional signals (S5, S4b) sit far below the 100-bet sample floor.

Everything downstream of missing data is left blank: three of seven correlation rows, every player-prop distribution, and every economic figure. Blanks are flagged, never filled.
