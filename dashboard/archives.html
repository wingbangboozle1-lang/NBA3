Why they were consolidated

Seven dashboards with seven ID schemes, seven navigations and four overlapping data registries meant a reviewer could not tell which numbers were canonical. See F-13 and suggestion SG-08.

The consolidated dashboard reuses the strongest work of the earlier sessions: the line-origination engine and its statistics module from origin/ — model/nbastats.py and model/build.py — now run from final/build_model.py against a refreshed slate.
