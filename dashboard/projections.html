Why most games show “no rule fires”

Only four of the eight candidate rules are slate-selectable (S4, S4b, S5, S5b). The other four apply to every rated game (S1, S2, S6) or need a rest differential that most games do not have (S3, n=17 out of 114). A game with no rule firing is a genuine result: the model has nothing candidate-worthy to say about it under the current rule set.

What a real pick would require

  1. A licensed, timestamped market price for the same fixture and market (blocking: F-03).
  2. A settlement rule book: regulation or including overtime, pushes, prop void rules (open: Q-10).
  3. A prediction cut-off and staleness rule, so a line is never published on stale availability data (open: Q-09, suggestion SG-13).
  4. A strategy that passes all seven gates. None does today (F-11).
  5. Resolution of the NBA Terms of Use position (blocking: F-01, F-02).
