How to read a row

  • Our spread (home) — the LM-C blend prediction of home margin, negated. Selected on out-of-sample RMSE (16.18 vs 17.30 baseline).
  • Our total — the T2 own-scoring estimator. Its walk-forward RMSE (20.35) is worse than the naive baseline (19.56). Treat it as a research output, not an edge.
  • P(home win) — normal CDF of the predicted margin over the residual SD. It assumes normal residuals and ignores injury uncertainty.
  • Strategies firing — which candidate rules select this game. Every one of them is under-powered; see the leaderboard.
  • Source — the official NBA game page for the fixture, plus the official team advanced table that produced the ratings.
