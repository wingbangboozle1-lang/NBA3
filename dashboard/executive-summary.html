Executive summary

Objective. Originate our own NBA lines, turn them into candidate strategies, and admit a strategy to a leaderboard only after it passes pre-declared validation gates that separate skill from luck.

What was built. A verified 174-game log for 2026-27 (2026-10-20 to 2026-11-12), two margin engines and two totals engines, a strictly chronological walk-forward harness (60-game burn-in, 114 out-of-sample games), seven validation gates, 8 candidate strategies, 22 originated lines for the next three days, a correlation module, an injury-to-minutes module, and a game-flow module. Every table row on this site links to the official NBA page it came from.

The three results that matter

16.18 vs 17.30Margin RMSE vs naive baseline (walk-forward, n=114). Better, but only 12.5% of variance explained.
20.35 vs 19.56Totals RMSE vs naive baseline. Worse. Not usable as an edge.
0 of 8Strategies passing all seven gates. Two are provisional and under-powered.

Luck was not removed by assertion. It was addressed structurally: expanding-window walk-forward with no look-ahead, a 60-game burn-in, an exact binomial test against the correct null (not 0.50 for parlays), Benjamini–Hochberg control at 0.05 across the strategy family, a split-half stability gate, an explicit minimum sample of 100 bets, and a required-n-for-80%-power figure printed next to every result. A short-run win rate is never presented as edge.

What is deliberately blank

  • Every economic figure. No odds feed, no licence, no price snapshots, so no expected value, return, or closing-line value is stated.
  • Three of seven correlation rows. Player-level dependence needs per-player game logs, which this project does not hold.
  • Every player-prop distribution. Only season averages and a point projection are available; a distribution is not.
  • Competition, venue and city for the published slate. Not printed on the official pages used.
  • Injury data for 11/15 and 11/16. Teams file by 5pm local the day before a game; those reports did not exist at capture time.
