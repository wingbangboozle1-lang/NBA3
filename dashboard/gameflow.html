Score state and rotation

Blowouts and close games change minutes in ways this project cannot yet measure: no play-by-play and no rotation data are held. What is measured is the overtime effect on the total: regulation games average 225.5 points, overtime games average 252.1. Nine of 174 games went to overtime.

Every total on the lines page includes overtime scoring, because that is how the source scores are recorded. If a settlement rule settles on regulation only, this is a material difference — see question Q-10.
