How values were verified

  • Copied, not derived. Team and player statistics are copied from the official NBA stats tables and compared cell by cell (570 and 310 checks respectively).
  • Reconciled. The game log is recomputed into games played, wins and losses for all 30 teams and compared with the official columns (91 checks).
  • Spot-checked directly. One date of final scores was compared score by score with the official games page.
  • Structurally checked. Slate and injury rows are checked for uniqueness, known team abbreviations, and official status vocabularies (334 checks).
  • Left blank when uncheckable. Where no official source exists for a value, the cell is blank and flagged. It is never filled in.
