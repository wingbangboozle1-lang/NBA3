Data & reproduction

Files, schemas and the commands that rebuild everything

Reproduce the whole site:

python3 -m integrate.engine
python3 -m integrate.build_site
python3 -m integrate.check

Standard library only - numpy, scipy and pandas are not available in this workspace, so statistics are implemented in model/nbastats.py (imported, not modified) and unit-tested there. The build is byte-deterministic: the permutation test is seeded (20261113) and no wall-clock timestamp is written into any artefact, so CI can assert that the committed pages match the committed code exactly.

Schema notes

  • orig_spread_home: our model's home spread in points, half-point rounded, negative = home favoured.
  • p_home_win: normal-CDF probability from the predicted margin and the MAE-derived residual scale.
  • minutes_at_risk_floor: sum of official season MPG for Out/Doubtful players present in the captured minutes table; a floor, never an estimate.
  • gradable_vs_own_line: false means the strategy's payoff cannot be measured against a price we set ourselves.

Not betting advice. Lines on this site are model output from verified NBA.com results, not market prices; no licensed odds feed exists in this repository. Strategy results are out-of-sample backtests on 114 games and none of them clears the eligibility gates. Follow local law and responsible-gambling practice.
