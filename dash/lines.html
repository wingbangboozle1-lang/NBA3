What the model is

Two independent rating engines are fitted on results alone (recency-weighted points for/against with league shrinkage, and a Massey ridge least-squares fit), and a game-by-game selector picks between them using only the 30 predictions that preceded it. That keeps model selection out of sample. Full detail and the variant table are on reconciliation.

Not betting advice. Lines on this site are model output from verified NBA.com results, not market prices; no licensed odds feed exists in this repository. Strategy results are out-of-sample backtests on 114 games and none of them clears the eligibility gates. Follow local law and responsible-gambling practice.
