A 2-leg same-game ticket on this sample pays out about 5.6% more often than independence implies - a real but small effect on 114 games, and the interval around it includes 1, so it is not yet a pricing rule. Cross-game dependence, which is what a 3- or 4-leg ticket actually needs, is left blank on purpose rather than assumed to be zero.
