Executive summary

Landing, headline numbers, what is and is not usable

Read this first

The model in this build forecasts the score, not the market. Out of sample its spread prediction is 6.7% better than a constant baseline on 114 games (paired t p=0.0497); its total prediction is 3.0% better and that part is not significant (p=0.1338). So we do have forecast skill on the margin, and we do not yet have evidence of betting skill: no bettor profits from a forecast, only from a forecast that beats a price, and no price exists in this repository. Everything below the fold is labelled accordingly.

How to read a number here

Every table row ends in a source. A dash (—) means the value was not verifiable and was left blank rather than estimated; hovering it says so. data/integration/model.json is the single artefact these pages render, so a row that looks wrong can be traced to the line of integrate/engine.py that produced it.

Not betting advice. Lines on this site are model output from verified NBA.com results, not market prices; no licensed odds feed exists in this repository. Strategy results are out-of-sample backtests on 114 games and none of them clears the eligibility gates. Follow local law and responsible-gambling practice.
