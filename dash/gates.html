Validation gates

What a strategy must clear before it is eligible, and who cleared what

Nine gates. A strategy must clear all of them to be eligible for the leaderboard; the gate set is also the answer to 'how do we remove luck', because gates 5-7 are nothing but probability statements about how often a run like this happens by chance.

G01 and G02 pass because provenance and leakage are properties of the pipeline, not of a strategy. G09 cannot be cleared by backtesting at all - it measures the live process, and it is the gate that catches everything a backtest hides.
