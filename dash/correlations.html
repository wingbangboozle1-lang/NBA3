A dependence ratio of 1.06 on 114 games is not a pricing rule; it is the first honest measurement of a quantity that was previously assumed. The number worth acting on is that it is *near* 1, which means same-game parlays are not as correlated as sportsbook risk models usually pretend - and that cross-game correlation, the one that matters for a 4-leg ticket, is still unknown.
