The RMSE columns are not comparable: different windows and three different baselines. Edge's baseline uses the population sd of all margins including the test games, which flatters its own improvement figure. The three published residual correlations (+0.023, +0.091, -0.018) differ by more than their point estimates while all three intervals contain zero - which is the right conclusion for a sample this size, and the wrong basis for a parlay price (E-F-04).
