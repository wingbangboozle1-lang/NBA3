ROI is measured at an assumed -110 against our own line because no market price exists (E-F-05); it is not a statement about beating a book. 0 of 12 gradable strategies clear the raw luck test, and each of those has fewer than 100 bets, which is precisely the trap the sample gate exists to catch: with 30 bets, a 75% hit rate is well within reach of luck even when the permutation p-value looks small.

What would move a row up

  • More games. A 55% strategy needs roughly 1,000 bets before the interval separates it from break-even.
  • A real price. Until a closing price exists, the leaderboard can rank forecast skill, never realised edge.
  • Pre-registration. Twelve strategies were tried here; without registering the rule and the metric in advance, the best-looking row is partly a selection artefact.
