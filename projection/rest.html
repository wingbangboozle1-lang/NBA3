Rest & travel

Rest and schedule density computed from the official schedule, with travel left blank rather than guessed.

Rest, back-to-back and 3-in-4 are arithmetic on the official schedule, not estimates: rest is the number of days between a team's previous game and this one. 8 back-to-backs and 8 3-in-4 situations occur across the 44 team-games in the window.

Travel distance is blank, on purpose. No official NBA page read in this session publishes arena latitude/longitude. The brief requires a SOURCED table, so the distances are left blank and flagged rather than estimated from city names. The distance column therefore prints — unverified for all 44 team-games. Feeding a guessed coordinate would make the column look complete and be wrong, which is the failure mode this project is built to avoid.
