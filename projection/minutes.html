Minutes & rotation

Top-eight minute shares, replacement shares, and an honest account of why the absence case cannot be forecast on this slate.

Minutes are modelled explicitly rather than assumed to stay constant: the top eight players by minutes take a published share of the rotation, and when a player is out his share is redistributed to the remaining seven in proportion to their own minutes, with the leftover assigned to replacements outside the top eight.

An overlap the project cannot paper over. Captured game logs cover 10 clubs from the 11/14 slate (BKN, CHI, CLE, DAL, DEN, DET, MEM, MIA, MIN, NOP). The archived injury report covers 11/15. The overlap — clubs that have both a captured log and an announced absence — is 8. So no real redistribution can be published for this slate. The table below is therefore a measured share model plus an explicitly labelled illustrative sensitivity, not a forecast.
