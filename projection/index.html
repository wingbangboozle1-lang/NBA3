What this surface is

The projection surface publishes research output: official captures, two independent estimators, their disagreement, rest and travel, rotation availability, and the gates that had to pass before any of it was published. Nothing here is a price, a stake or a recommendation.

Separation. Gated picks live on a different surface with a different index. The two are never mixed in one table: a reader here cannot mistake a sealed bet for a projection. The only cross-reference is the banner at the top of this page. Open the bet surface.

The headline confidence signal

The primary signal is not a blend and not a single rating: it is the disagreement between two estimators built on different information (E1 uses final margins, E2 uses only win/loss). On the walk-forward sample they disagree by a mean of 10.73 percentage points and land on opposite sides of the moneyline in 14.8% of games. The 50/50 blend is published underneath, as a blend, and its log-loss (0.6160) is shown next to both constituents so the reader can see that it is a small improvement over E1 alone, not a different model.

Luck, stated as a z-score. No team is currently outside the ±1.96 noise band (largest |z| = 1.42). That is a real result and it means the league table on 2026-11-15 is not yet distinguishable from noise at the 5% level. A strategy leaning on any team's over-performance would have to clear that band first; none does, so none is published.
