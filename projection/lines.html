Why the total is not an average of scoring averages

The model total is possessions × points per possession, where each team's pace and efficiency are matched against the opponent's defence. The last-but-one column is the naive benchmark that this project was told not to use — the sum of each team's own points per game — printed beside it so the two can be compared on every row instead of being asserted apart.
