Settlement and sealed ledger

Verified finals, the settlement stamp, the ledger chain, and every pick that is still open.

Settlement runs as soon as a game day ends, and only from a final that two official reads agree on. One game qualified: IND @ PHI on 11/15. Its settlement is in the sealed ledger with the stamp of the moment the final was confirmed — not the build's clock.

The rule this page enforces. A final is settled only when the nba.com game page and the official game book PDF print the same score, and the printed quarter scores sum to that final for both teams. A scoreboard snapshot with a live clock is not a final and is never graded.
