What this project still cannot claim

  • No edge against a price. There is no licensed closing line in the repository, so every "edge" is the model compared with itself.
  • No validated strategy. Three strategies are pre-registered and eleven picks are sealed; one has settled. A leaderboard over one settled pick ranks nothing.
  • No settlement rule for a postponed game. As written, such a pick can never close.
  • No official arena coordinates. The travel table is a labelled reference source.
  • No rotation forecast. The minutes model is a demonstration until a captured game contains a player who later appears as Out.
