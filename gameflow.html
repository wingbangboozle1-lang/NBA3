Analysis

  • The schedule dates in slates.csv let us count rest situations, such as the Nov 14 and Nov 15 games for teams in consecutive slates. This is a schedule fact, not a measured rest effect.
  • Score margin and foul trouble are the factors most likely to move starter minutes, but no data here tests that.
