What this session found

The published injury snapshot is stale by 8h15m and 24 report versions. The dashboard publishes the 05:15 AM ET report (49 rows). The official index now lists the November 14 reports through 1:30 PM ET, and the 1:30 PM ET report has 69 rows. The 01:45 PM probe returns AccessDenied, so 1:30 PM is the newest that exists.
Four of the five 11/14 team-games published as NOT YET SUBMITTED have filed. NOP, CLE, CHI and MIA now list players; only MEM is still blank, and for MEM that is correct - its 1:00 PM local deadline is 2:00 PM ET, which had not arrived.
And yet not one published number moves. The engine was re-run with the refreshed report substituted: 0 of 17 price fields changed across 27 lines and the pick list is identical. The refresh changes the absence roll-up printed beside the line, not the line. That is the finding, not a footnote - see RR-05.
