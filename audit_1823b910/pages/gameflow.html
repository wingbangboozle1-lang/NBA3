Reading the flow

  • Four of the five home teams are on the second night of a back-to-back (NOP, CLE, MIA, MEM); in CHI@MIN it is the visitor, Chicago. That is why those five teams were the ones the published report could not yet show.
  • Rest differentials on this slate are 1 or 2 days - there is no rested-vs-rusted split here, so the rest signal has nothing to bite on tonight.
  • Projected pace spans 99.8 to 102.5 possessions, a range of 2.6 - small enough that pace is not separating these games.
  • DAL@CLE is the one game where the refresh changes the picture materially: Cleveland filed with Donovan Mitchell and James Harden both Questionable and Evan Mobley Out, and none of that reaches the published spread.
