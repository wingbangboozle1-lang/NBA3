Convention. Vacated = players listed Out or Doubtful (the engine's own convention in scripts/strategy_lab.py::rollup). At risk = players listed Questionable; they are NOT counted as absent. Both sums use only official season per-game figures the repository has captured (top 31 by minutes per game), so every total is a FLOOR.
Unknowns. A player with no captured official minutes figure is counted in players_without_official_minutes and contributes 0, never an estimate. A team that had not filed produces None, never 0.
What is deliberately not modelled. Who absorbs the vacated minutes, and what share of the vacated production the replacement returns, is NOT modelled. It needs per-player game logs and on/off splits, which this repository does not have. Publishing a replacement rate would be a guess, so the cell is left blank.

Why most vacated minutes read as zero

The only official per-player minutes capture in this repository is the top 31 by minutes per game. Most players named on an injury report are rotation-depth or two-way players and fall outside it, so they contribute zero to the floor rather than an invented number. The single team whose vacuum is non-zero is MIN, because Anthony Edwards is in that table.
