How to reproduce this

python3 audit_dd1eb1f1/checks.py
python3 audit_dd1eb1f1/analysis.py
python3 audit_dd1eb1f1/build.py

Neither checks.py nor analysis.py touches the network: they read the captures in audit_dd1eb1f1/data/ and the artefacts already in the repository, so a reader with the checkout gets the same numbers. The captures themselves were transcribed from the official pages listed on each page.

What this audit does not cover

  • Team and player statistics were not re-read: stats.nba.com is not reachable from this workspace. The schedule pages and the injury PDFs are.
  • The 183-game results log was checked for internal consistency, not re-transcribed game by game. A full re-transcription is a separate job.
  • No market odds were compared, because no licensed odds source is connected to this repository. Every price on the site is our own originated number.
