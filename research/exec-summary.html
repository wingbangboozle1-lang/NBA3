Executive summary

What is verified, what is blocked, and what is needed. Every number is computed from the data files.

  • Slates. 31 games are verified against the official schedule for Nov 13 to 16, 2026 (ET). Nov 13 has 9 games, Nov 14 has 5, Nov 15 has 9, and Nov 16 has 8. Every game ID, venue and preview link matches the schedule page (slates table).
  • Injuries. The 11/13/26 11:00 AM ET report is transcribed: 89 player rows and 9 team-status rows. Of the player rows, 63 are Out and 16 are Questionable. 27 rows have no matchup cell and 14 have no team cell in the PDF text; those cells are left blank (injury table).
  • Originated lines. Not produced. No timestamped odds, prices or historical outcomes are available from an official source reached here (Q-DATA-01 and Q-DATA-02).
  • Picks. None published. The picks table lists all 31 games with blank lines and picks, pending legal advice on Dutch promotion rules (picks).
  • Strategies and gates. 11 strategy hypotheses are defined. 11 of 11 are blocked. Gate thresholds are proposals (strategies, gates).
  • Correlations. None measured. Each cell is blank by design (correlations).
  • Rotation and game flow. A tested function redistributes minutes when players are Out, and a 7-factor game-flow list is documented. Neither gives a published minutes or production figure, because no minutes data is reachable (game flow).
  • Legal. NBA Terms s.9 bars use of NBA Statistics in connection with any gambling activity. s.19 selects New York law. Dutch law (Art. 1 of the Wet op de kansspelen) restricts promotion of unlicensed gambling. The minimum online age is not verified and is not published (flagged items).
  • Repository. No licence. Pages still builds from the branch root (main /), so the whole public repository is served; the Pages source switch failed with a permission error in this session (repo review).
