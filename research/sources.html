Analysis

  • NBA sources: schedule, injury reports and Terms of Use were read. Statistics pages were reachable but returned no data tables.
  • Dutch sources: the statute and the Ksa summaries were read. The Ksa summary is not binding, so statute text governs.
  • NIST: three method pages were read directly. The earlier DOI citation was not read and has been removed.
