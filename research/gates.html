Validation gates

Eleven validation gates. A strategy needs every gate to pass. None can pass without data.

Overall status is BLOCKED for every strategy. Missing evidence is reported as BLOCKED, never PASS (pipeline/gates.py). Thresholds are not approved and are not evidence.
