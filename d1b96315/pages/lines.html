How to read the columns

  • |A−B| is the headline the brief asks for: the disagreement between two independent estimators. Under 2.5 points the row is marked agreeing; over the frozen 5-point cap the row is refused a bet flag, with the reason printed inside the cell.
  • rotation availability is its own column, as required. applied means an Out/Doubtful club's minutes were redistributed and the production cost charged to the team; no rotation data means we hold no official box score for that club and refuse to model it; nothing to apply means the club filed and printed no Out/Doubtful row. The three states are different facts and are never collapsed into one.
  • rest is days since the club's last game, counting verified finals and the official slate; b2b and 3-in-4 follow the definitions on the method page.
  • travel is great-circle arena-to-arena kilometres. The arena name is checked against the official schedule on every row and a mismatch is printed in the cell; the coordinates come from a general-reference table on main, which is why the status line sits under the number.
  • luck z is how far a club's record sits from what its efficiency predicts; |z| > 2 refuses the row.
  • injury filing states, per club, whether the newest revision carries rows, the NOT YET SUBMITTED marker, or nothing at all for that date. The 11/18 rows read outside window because the report runs two days ahead — a fact about the report, not about the players.
