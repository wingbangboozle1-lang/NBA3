Known, unknown, and how to close each one

Ten items, each stating whether the answer is in hand, what the measurement says, and what specifically would turn the unknown into a number. Nothing here is a request for a value the site could look up itself — each one needs either a decision or a feed.

Q01 · closing line

unknown Is there a licensed or otherwise usable closing-line feed, and which one?

Nothing on the bet surface can be scored without it: the model-anchored prior, the log-loss weight and the ROI of every strategy all depend on a price we do not have. The official games page renders no odds in the fetched HTML.

To find out: Owner supplies the feed (or the policy for entering published closing lines by hand). A one-file capture job, the same shape as the injury capture, is enough to start: date, game, market, line, source URL, retrieval stamp.

Q02 · injury refresh schedule

known Should the refresh job chase the newest revision on game day?

Yes, and it currently cannot: the site published 2026-11-16 09:15 AM ET while the index already listed a 01:15 PM ET revision. The cron is 15 5,11,17,23 — nothing fires between 11:15 and 17:15 UTC, i.e. across the whole NBA afternoon.

To find out: Add a game-day pass ~30 minutes before the earliest tip in a block, and let the job take the newest item in the index rather than a constructed filename.
