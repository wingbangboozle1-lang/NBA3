p(favourite covers our line) = Phi(|edge| / residual sd); p(over our total) = Phi(|our total - league mean total| / sd total)
Gaussian: p_parlay = Phi2(Phi^-1(p1), Phi^-1(p2); rho). The naive price p1 x p2 is printed beside it on every row, so the correlation effect is visible, not hidden.
both rhos are measured on the verified finals in this repo: margin against total, and the margin of one game against the margin of another game on the same date. Nothing is assumed from another league or season.

What the numbers say

  • The measured ρ between a game's margin and its total is -0.2113 — negative, and its interval -0.574 to 0.22 straddles zero at n=23. So the honest statement is "we measured a small negative dependence and we cannot yet separate it from noise". The pricing effect is -3.33 to -0.77 probability points, which is printed rather than hidden, and it is smaller than the residual sd of the model itself.
  • Cross-game dependence — the thing that makes a multi-leg slip dangerous — measured -0.0492 (-0.263 to 0.17) across 82 same-date pairs. It is near zero, which is why no same-date multi-leg is priced here as if it were safe: near zero with an interval that wide is "unknown", not "independent".
  • Both ρ and both prices are ours. There is no closing line to compare against, so none of it can be scored as skill yet (gate G8; Q01).

Game-flow measurement that feeds the total column: share of all points scored by quarter on the 13 games with official quarter scores: Q1 0.2476 · Q2 0.2382 · Q3 0.2747 · Q4 0.2395 · sum 1.0.
