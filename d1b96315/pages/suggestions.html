Four paths, pick one before anything is implemented

Each path is a single decision with its cost, what it unlocks, and what it costs in re-work. They are ordered by how much of the brief they unblock per hour of owner time, not by how nice they sound.

Path 1 Pin home court to the league-wide measurement, everywhere

cost: one constant, one re-seal

Do Adopt 0.9 points (measured on 196 home games) in the round model, the app estimators and the ledger, and record the old value as history rather than overwriting it.

Buy The current three-way split (0.90 / 4.12 / 1.62) is the largest single disagreement in the repo and it moves every home line. A pin built on 196 games beats one built on 23.

Pay Every already-sealed entry keeps its old constant — that is the point of the chain — so two published surfaces will show different numbers for one game day unless the choice is stated on the page. Half a day of work: the constant, the note, a re-run of the two builders.

Otherwise Do nothing: keep publishing three numbers and let readers find the 3.2-point swing themselves.

Path 2 Get a price feed, even a manual one

cost: one capture job + one table

Do A closing-line table with date, game, market, line, source, stamp — captured the same way the injury PDFs are. Manual entry of published closing numbers is acceptable if it is stamped and sourced, because it is then a source like any other.

Buy It unlocks G8, which blocks all five strategies today, and turns the model-anchored prior from a null field into a measured log-loss weight. Without it no line on this site can ever be scored as skill.

Pay Nothing in the model changes, so nothing published now is invalidated. The bet surface stays empty until the depth gate clears as well, so expect 'we can finally measure', not 'we can bet'.

Otherwise Wait for a licensed feed: cleaner, but the board has listed this gap for several sessions and a stamped manual table costs one afternoon.

Path 3 Capture one game book per game, every day

cost: six to ten PDFs a day

Do Extend the capture job with the official per-game book (the same URL pattern already used for the ATL@MIL re-read). That gives per-player minutes for all 30 clubs, real top-8 shares, and the quarter vectors for every game instead of 13 of 23.

Buy It moves the availability model from 9 clubs to 30, makes player-prop work possible, and gives game-flow its own verified input instead of a partial one. It also makes the I2 quarter gate testable on a real population.

Pay The transcripts are large: keep them under the session tree or a data path, not in <code>app/</code>, and expect the PDF renderer's column-shift artefact on every one of them — parse by content and count the unread cells, as this tree does.

Otherwise Keep covering the handful of games a session happens to re-read: cheaper, and it is why six clubs on tonight's slate have no modelled availability at all.

Path 4 Chase the newest revision on game day

cost: one cron line + one index read

Do Read the report index, take the newest item, and add a pre-tip pass (~30 min before the earliest tip of a block). The freshness gate then measures a gap that is actually the league's latest document.

Buy Today's published snapshot was about 4 h 05 m behind, and the missing rows were the ones that mattered: 19 rows appeared when four clubs filed, including a league suspension and two starters moved to Out.

Pay The gate already exists; this changes what it measures, so more rows will legitimately fail the rebuild until the job keeps up. Expect the red-build count to rise before it falls.

Otherwise Keep the four fixed crons: simple, and it is what left the 01:15 PM filing unpublished today.
