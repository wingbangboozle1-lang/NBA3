Estimator B — possessions × points per possession, with availability

poss = mean(possessions per game of the two clubs); pts_t = poss x ((off_rtg_t - availability_delta_t) + def_rtg_opp) / 2 / 100; margin = pts_home - pts_away + pinned hca; total = pts_home + pts_away

The total is built from possessions and efficiency, never from adding two scoring averages: a scoring average already contains the pace a team actually played and the opponents it faced, so two of them added together describe no single game. Both totals are printed on every row of the lines page with their gap, and a scoring average already contains the pace the team actually played and the opponent it faced; adding two of them makes a total that no single game implies. Both are printed for every row so the difference is auditable.

The availability model, stated exactly

  1. Take the club's most recent captured official box score (a game-book PDF) and each player's minutes in it. The top-8 minutes shares are what the club actually ran, not a depth chart.
  2. Remove the players the newest official report prints as Out or Doubtful. Questionable and Day-To-Day are not removed: they are game-time decisions, and treating them as absent would let the model pretend to know something the league has not said.
  3. Redistribute the removed minutes pro rata to the minutes each remaining player actually played — that is the replacement share, printed per player in lines.json.
  4. Charge the production cost: each replacement's points-per-minute from that same box, applied to the minutes added; the loss against the block that was removed becomes a points-per-game delta, divided by the club's official possessions per game to become an efficiency delta, and estimator B uses that delta in its own rating term.
Where it stops. 21 of the 52 rows in the new box transcripts have a stat cell the PDF extraction dropped, so their points-per-minute is unread and the affected club's adjustment is published as minutes-only or not at all. 6 of 16 clubs playing tonight have no box score anywhere in the repository, so "no rotation data" is printed instead of a zero (D-08).
