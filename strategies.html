The validation gates

A strategy is only allowed onto the live leaderboard if it clears all of these. The gates are deliberately strict: the point of the exercise is to remove luck, not to find a story that fits 114 games.

GateRequirementWhy
G1 provenanceEvery input is an official NBA figure or arithmetic on one Blocks anything trained on a number we cannot show a source for.
G2 no look-aheadEvery prediction made with only games played strictly before it A model that has seen the future will always look good.
G3 sample sizeAt least 100 graded bets Below 100, a 55% hit rate cannot be separated from noise at 80% power.
G4 powerReport the n the observed effect would actually need Shows how far from adequate the sample is, in the same units as G3.
G5 raw significanceTwo-sided p < 0.05 against the correct null Exact binomial test. For parlays the null is the independence-implied joint rate, not 0.50.
G6 multiple testingSurvives Benjamini-Hochberg at FDR 0.05 Eight candidates were searched; the bar has to rise accordingly.
G7 stabilityDirection of the edge holds in both halves of the sample An effect that only exists in one half is usually a calendar artefact.

Reading the result honestly

Nothing passes. That is the finding, and it is the most useful thing this page contains. Two strategies are worth naming:

  • S5 — fade our own totals of 235+. 21 of 24 unders went under (p=0.0003). The threshold was pre-specified at 235 before the backtest ran, which is the right thing to do, but 24 bets is roughly a fifth of the sample the gate demands, and the effect is exactly what regression to the mean produces when a model is constructed from noisy season averages. It fails G3 and, at 56 bets needed for 80% power, it is nowhere close.
  • S4b — back road favourites of 8+. 20 of 28 covers (p=0.0357), but it does not survive Benjamini-Hochberg correction across the eight candidates (G6), so we do not treat it as established.

Neither result should be traded. Both should be re-run automatically once the season supplies the sample they need; the pipeline already does this with no code changes.
