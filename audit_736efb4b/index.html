6. Method and limits

  • Capture: verbatim fetch of the PDF text; transcription by audit_736efb4b/capture.py. The status counts of the transcription equal the raw counts of each status word in the report text (true).
  • Comparison: audit_736efb4b/compare.py. Rebuild: python3 audit_736efb4b/capture.py && python3 audit_736efb4b/compare.py && python3 audit_736efb4b/build.py.
  • Tests: python3 -m unittest discover -s audit_736efb4b -p 'test_*.py' (counts, official-link rule, blank-not-filled rule).
  • The official text extraction loses column headers after page 1. This is why several rows print no date, matchup or team.
