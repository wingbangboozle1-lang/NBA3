Questions that must be answered before any of this is funded

  1. Is a closing-line feed available? Without it the prior cannot be anchored and every line stays ours alone. Until then no strategy should be described as tested against a market.
  2. How many out-of-sample games does E2 need before it earns weight? The pre-registration says 30. The efficiency page only produces one new observation per game day, so that is roughly a month of real time.
  3. Do the five clubs with no captured book get one? Their games are on the schedule; the books are published the following day. The capture list is mechanical.
  4. What counts as "skill" for the leaderboard? A strategy has to beat the coin-flip log-loss of 0.6931 on games it never saw, not on the games it was fitted to.
  5. Who owns the settlement if a game book and the standings page disagree? Both are official. The rule today is that the game book's own quarters must sum to its printed final and the standings page must show the matching streak.
