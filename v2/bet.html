The gated surface. Empty, correctly.

This is the gated surface. A strategy appears as a bet only if it passes every gate: sufficient sample, adequate power, exact-binomial significance, false-discovery control across the whole board, half-to-half stability, an edge that clears the price actually paid, an edge above the board-wide luck threshold, a pre-registration hash recorded before the first bet, and a real market price.

There are no bets on this page, and that is the correct result. No licensed market price is connected to this repository, so the final gate fails for every rule by construction. Every edge on the board is measured against the project's own originated line and an assumed -110, which can tell you whether our own line is biased but cannot tell you whether a bookmaker would have paid.
