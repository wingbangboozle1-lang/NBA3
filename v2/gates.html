What the freshness gate caught

The 6:15 AM injury report was captured, parsed and ready to price the slate. While the build was running, the official index started listing a 6:30 AM report. The gate refused the build, the newer document was captured, and the rebuild reads it by name — not "whichever file is newest", which would let an old report ride a new timestamp.

The difference between the two revisions was a single spelling correction: the league fixed "Embid" to "Embiid". Zero status changes. That distinction is published rather than flattened, because a diff that reads a typo fix as one player vanishing and another appearing invents two roster events out of a proofreading pass.
