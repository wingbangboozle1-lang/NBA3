Overview

Overview

Two estimators, published disagreement, gated strategies.

This is the second pipeline. It exists because the first one had a single model and a single number, and a single number cannot tell you whether it is right or whether it is lucky. Here there are two independent estimators, the gap between them is the headline, and the blend is published underneath.

Where the numbers stand. 13 of 13 source and freshness gates pass. 18 strategies are registered and backtested. 0 may be published as a projection and 0 may be offered as a bet.

The two estimators

They are independent in the way that matters: different inputs, different arithmetic, no shared quantity. If both read the same rating they would agree by construction and the disagreement — the thing this pipeline is for — would always be zero.

Why B's number is not a result. B is fitted on one team-statistics snapshot dated 2026-11-15 and used to price all 131 games played before that date, so its error carries information from the future. It cannot be quoted as an out-of-sample figure and no rule that reads B is ranked. The fix is a per-date statistics history; until then A at 15.23 points is the only honest accuracy claim on this site.

Home-court advantage is pinned, not fitted

The brief asks for HCA pinned to the measured home-win rate. That is what happens: a free regression coefficient is not used anywhere.
