The promotion rule

ControlRule
scoringBrier score of our_model_prob against the settled outcome, plus a calibration table by probability bucket.
luck_controlA strategy is promoted out of the ledger only when its Brier skill score against a home-rate baseline has a bootstrap 95% interval that excludes zero.
minimum_sample100 settled picks, chosen before any settlement was observed.
immutabilitySettled rows are never edited. A correction is appended as a new row that supersedes the old, so the audit trail survives.
