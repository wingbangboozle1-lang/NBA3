Claims that are not verified

IDClaimStatusWhySource
CLM-10STRAT1 is a spread strategyDISCREPANCYThe recorded settlement rule is `won = (margin > 0) if side == HOME else (margin < 0)`. That settles on who wins the game, not on covering a spread. It is a moneyline rule. The published 77.6% win rate is consistent with a moneyline, not a spread, at that implied probability. See contradiction C-03.source
CLM-11STRAT1 and STRAT2 are profitable after an assumed -110 priceDISCREPANCYArithmetic reproduces. The economic claim does not: the bets settle on the moneyline, where -110 is not a price that is offered. Recorded model probabilities imply fair prices near -332 and +105. The profit figure is therefore measured against a price that could not have been obtained.source
CLM-12Parlay example: 0.58 x 0.58 legs at rho = 0.3 gives joint 0.58, fair price 1.72DISCREPANCYThe published figure is wrong. The joint probability of two 0.58 legs cannot reach 0.58 unless the legs are perfectly correlated; 0.58 is the Frechet upper bound. Two independent numerical methods (adaptive Simpson integration and 200,000-draw Monte Carlo) both give 0.3832 at rho = +0.3, implying fair odds 2.61 rather than 1.72. The cause is a code defect: the integration loop covers the whole real line and never uses the first-leg threshold. See contradiction C-02.source
CLM-13Same-game margin/total correlation -0.018UNVERIFIEDRecomputed Pearson r between margin and total on the walk-forward test set. Signed value depends on which test set is used; magnitude is near zero in every version. Cannot be confirmed from an official source because it is a model-internal statistic.source
CLM-19The 5:00 PM report is the current injury snapshotDISCREPANCYA 7:30 PM report for the same date is published at the official index and was available before the 5:00 PM snapshot was committed. Between the two, 11 players changed status, including Klay Thompson Probable to Out and Pelle Larsson Doubtful to Out. See contradiction C-05.source
