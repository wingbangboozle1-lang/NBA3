Why the old number was impossible. For two 0.58 legs the joint probability cannot leave [0.16, 0.58]. The old published value sat exactly on the upper bound, which is only possible at rho = 1. The previously published 0.58 was therefore not a small error — it was the mathematical ceiling, reachable only if the two legs were the same event.

An honest note on one check that was wrong

While auditing, this session initially flagged the /edge/ test-set size of 138 as inconsistent with a 30-game burn-in, because 30 + 138 = 168 rather than 174. That inference was wrong. The walk-forward file has 36 rows without a fitted calibration (the logistic fit needs 30 games and then fails to converge for 6 more), and 138 is exactly the number of rows that have both a fitted calibration and an index of at least 30. The false alarm is kept in the claim ledger rather than quietly deleted, because the lesson is the point: inspection produced a false positive, and recomputation corrected it.
