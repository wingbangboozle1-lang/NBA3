Injury verification

The published injury table was checked row by row against the official NBA PDF, then checked again against a later official report to test whether it was current.

98named players in the official reporttranscribed verbatim
110matched to our table0 status mismatches
117rows in our table7 are team-level placeholders
11players whose status changed within 2.5 hoursin the 07:30 PM report
The transcription itself is clean. Every one of the 98 named players in the official 05:00 PM report appears in data/injuries.json with the same status and the same matchup. Zero mismatches. The rows our table adds beyond the named players are team-level "NOT YET SUBMITTED" headers, which the PDF does contain as team rows.
The snapshot, however, was already stale when it was published. The official index publishes a report every 15 minutes. A 07:30 PM report exists, 2.5 hours after the 05:00 PM snapshot the site uses, and 11 players changed status in it. Two of them went to Out. That is exactly the kind of change that moves a line, and the site could not have shown it.

Why this matters more than it looks. The PDF text extraction drops the team and matchup cells for some rows, so 18 rows could only be attributed with reference to the surrounding context. Those rows are marked U and left blank rather than guessed. A further 19 carry a team printed on the PDF but a matchup inferred from the official schedule; they are marked S. A reader can therefore tell, on every single row, what was printed by the league and what was added by us.
