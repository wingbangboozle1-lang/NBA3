CLM-12/edge/Parlay example: 0.58 x 0.58 legs at rho = 0.3 gives joint 0.58, fair price 1.720.58The published figure is wrong. The joint probability of two 0.58 legs cannot reach 0.58 unless the legs are perfectly correlated; 0.58 is the Frechet upper bound. Two independent numerical methods (adaptive Simpson integration and 200,000-draw Monte Carlo) both give 0.3832 at rho = +0.3, implying fair odds 2.61 rather than 1.72. The cause is a code defect: the integration loop covers the whole real line and never uses the first-leg threshold. See contradiction C-02.DISCREPANCYsource
