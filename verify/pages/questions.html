Both were read directly in prior sessions and the quoted requirement is reproduced in the flagged register.Whether this project offers, promotes or merely analyses betting content, and from which jurisdiction it is operated. Whether the owner holds or will obtain a Koa licence. Whether the NBA terms prohibit even a private, non-monetised model.Written legal opinion covering (a) the licence question for the intended jurisdiction and (b) the NBA terms question. Nothing in this repository can substitute for it.Yes - blocks every staking recommendation, and blocks any public leaderboard that ranks real money outcomes.source · also

Q-03Which market prices may be recorded?One pipeline (/lab/) records a single-book DraftKings price relayed by an ESPN endpoint and classifies it TRUSTED-2, explicitly 'not an NBA source'. Also whether a sportsbook's terms permit storing and republishing their prices.Procure a feed (or a documented free source) and record the licence terms. Then re-run the backtests with real prices. Until then no ROI figure in this repository is an economic result.Yes - without it, profitability cannot be measured, only self-consistency.source
