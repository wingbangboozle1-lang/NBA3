How each estimator is built

  • A · form. The official net rating, taken at face value. It is what the club has actually out-scored opponents by, per 100 possessions.
  • B · structure. Rebuilt from shooting efficiency, turnover rate and offensive rebounding — the four factors — and compared with the same reconstruction for what the club allows. It shares no input with A.
  • Centring. Both are expressed as points per 100 possessions above their own league mean (A: +0.0233, B: +12.9140). Without this, a constant inside B would appear as disagreement between methods when it is only a level shift.
