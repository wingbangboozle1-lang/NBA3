Slate & totals

The slate is the official schedule for today and the next two days. Totals are modelled as possessions × points per possession — and this page states plainly where that formula does and does not differ from the naive sum.

Disclosure: on season-wide inputs the two agree

The official offensive rating is exactly 100 × points / possessions. So possessions × points-per-possession reproduces the sum of the two clubs' scoring averages to within a tenth of a point. The formula is the correct object and is what the model uses; it cannot yet do additional work because opponent-specific rates need per-game logs. That identity is pinned by gate I10, not hidden.
