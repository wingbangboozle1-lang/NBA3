The totals model

Expected total = shared possessions × (points per possession, away + home). Both clubs play the same number of possessions in a game, so the game's possession count is the mean of the two clubs' pace. Points per possession is the official offensive rating divided by 100. The naive alternative — adding the two clubs' season scoring averages — is printed beside every total, and the identity between them on season-wide inputs is disclosed on the Slate page.

Rules the pipeline will not break

  • No repair. A document that contradicts itself is published with both readings and the row is withheld.
  • No inference. A cell the document does not print is null and counted.
  • No positional attribution. Cells are classified by content; a matchup is never inherited across a page break.
  • No silent splice. A row split by a page break is merged, counted and tested.
  • No clock in the artefacts. The same inputs reproduce the same bytes, so CI diffs the tree.
