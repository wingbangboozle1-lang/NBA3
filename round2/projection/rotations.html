Rotation availability, per side

"Vacated minutes" is the sum of the captured mean minutes of the players the official report prints as Out or Doubtful, where a captured mean exists; minutes are then redistributed one-for-one to the club's next-ranked men from the same captured books. A player whose captured games show him already inactive contributes zero, and is reported as already_inactive_in_captured_games rather than rounded up into an injury impact. Points impact is not estimated: no captured source prints a per-minute scoring rate for a bench player, so that field is blank everywhere.
