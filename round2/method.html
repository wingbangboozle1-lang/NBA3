How a line is made here

  1. Possessions, not averages. A total is pace × points-per-possession, with pace from the official advanced page and each side's ppp as the mean of its own rate and its opponent's allowed rate. An average of two points-per-game figures is never used: it silently double-counts pace.
  2. Home court is pinned. 0.76 points, which is probit(0.5178) × the log's margin s.d. 16.97. The standings' own HOME splits give 0.87 independently; with the one neutral-site game removed from the denominator the two agree to 0.87 exactly.
  3. Two estimators, disagreement as the headline. A = season aggregates; B = the verified game log, recomputed only from games before tip. The published confidence signal is |A − B|; the blend sits underneath at equal weight, with no optimisation against outcomes.
  4. Rest is measured, not assumed. Back-to-back games in this log have been outscored by -1.40 per game on average and rested games by 1.49, a gap of 2.89 points with a standard error of 2.91 (z = 0.99). That is why the adjustment is applied at half weight per side, and why a strategy built on it is refused by its own frozen z-floor.
  5. Luck is a z-score. Actual minus Pythagenpat-expected wins, standardised; 0 clubs outside ±2 tonight.
  6. Correlation is measured and then priced. Same-night margin rho is 0.012 on 808 game pairs, CI95 [-0.057, 0.081]; totals -0.033, home-win indicators -0.013. Parlays are priced with this rho and the naive product is printed next to them on the bet surface.
  7. Minutes are modelled per man. Out/Doubtful players lose their captured mean minutes and the club's next-ranked men absorb them one-for-one; points impact stays blank because no captured source prints a bench per-minute scoring rate.
  8. The market anchor is off. A closing-line blend needs a closing line and a measured out-of-sample log-loss to weight it; the feed reports "not connected" with 0 rows, so the weight is unestimated and the blended line is blank on every row.

Freshness and provenance, as the job enforces them

The scheduled job fetches, gates, and only then rebuilds. Gates in this round: 14/14 pass, and each is recomputed from the artefacts on disk rather than recorded by hand — see the gate board. The injury gate compares the newest report's printed revision time against the game day being priced; the provenance gate requires an https NBA source on every published row; the settlement gate requires a verified official final per game.
