## Verification log

Every published row carries a source URL and a verification tier. Tiers:

- **OFFICIAL** — operated by the NBA (`nba.com`, `official.nba.com`, `ak-static.cms.nba.com`, `cdn.nba.com`).
- **TRUSTED-2°** — reputable secondary (`basketball-reference.com`, `espn.com`/`espncdn.com`). Not the league's own number; used only where the official source is not machine-readable, and always labelled in the UI.
- **DERIVED** — arithmetic on the two tiers above by `scripts/`. Formulas documented in `docs/model-methodology.md`.
- **UNVERIFIED** — no source found; value left blank and listed on `review.html`.
