Leaderboard rules (proposal only)

Pre-registration is proposed as SG-04: a primary metric (for example Brier or log loss against a no-vig market baseline, closing-line value, or net ROI with a bootstrap interval), a minimum sample, a horizon, and thresholds. Owner-set values are required before any evaluation. No thresholds are set here.

Parlay and combo policy

  • A parlay's probability is not the product of its legs unless independence is tested (G7).
  • Same-game correlations are unmeasured. Blank by policy (7C-F-06).
  • Player-prop combos need minutes data (7C-F-05) and price data (7C-F-03).
