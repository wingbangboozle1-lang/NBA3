Injury Impact

How injuries move rotation minutes and, through minutes, production — with the parts we could not verify left blank.

Method: from a status to minutes to production

The official NBA injury report gives a status, not a minutes number. To turn one into the other we need the player's minutes, and the only official minutes table we captured is the season players/traditional leaderboard, which holds the top 31 players by minutes. So:

  1. A player is matched by name against that official minutes table.
  2. His status is converted to an availability weight: Out 0.00, Doubtful 0.25, Questionable 0.60, Probable 0.90, Available 1.00. These weights are our assumption, not an official figure — they are listed on the flagged page.
  3. Minutes at risk = season MPG × (1 − weight). The team total is the quantified minutes vacuum.
  4. Production impact is projected by scaling season per-game production by the ratio of projected to season minutes.

Players who are on an official report but not in the captured minutes table get a blank minutes cell and an UNVERIFIED badge. We do not estimate their minutes. As the season goes on this gap is the single biggest thing to fix, and it is ranked first on the site-improvement page.
