Reading the table

  • A margin is printed from the away club's point of view; negative means the home club is projected to win by that many points.
  • "n/m" means the estimator could not be computed for that game — never zero, and never an estimate carried over from a neighbouring row.
  • Disagreement is |A − B| in points. It is the published confidence signal because the two estimators use disjoint inputs: season aggregates versus the game-by-game log.
  • No price, no stake and no parlay appears on this page. Those live on the bet surface, which is gated and currently empty.
