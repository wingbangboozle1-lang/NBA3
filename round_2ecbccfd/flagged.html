Open questions for the owner

  1. Which licensed feed should supply closing lines, given that the NBA's terms bar using its own statistics for betting? Without it the market anchor stays blank forever — is a no-anchor policy acceptable, or should the anchor come from a non-NBA market source with its own provenance page?
  2. Should the ledger's settlement run be allowed to settle a game whose quarter table conflicts with its final, as this round does for margin and total but not for quarters? The alternative is to void the game entirely, which loses one game of sample per occurrence.
  3. Who owns the arena-coordinate table? It is the only non-official input in the pipeline and it is load-bearing for travel, which is currently reported but not priced.
  4. Is the 15-minute rotation-player floor (used to separate a rotation absence from a bench absence) the right threshold, or should it be defined by the captured box score's own first-eight ranking?
