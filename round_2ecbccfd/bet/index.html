Gated surface — nothing here is published for betting. Two independent reasons, both stated in the repository. First, the NBA's own terms (clause 9(iv) of the site terms) bar using NBA statistics for gambling purposes, so this project's bet surface stays behind a gate by policy. Second, and independently, no strategy has passed its validation gates, so there is nothing that this round would fund even if the first reason did not apply. 3 strategies are refused before tip by their own frozen thresholds; the sealed picks on the ledger are projections, graded without money.

What would have to change before this surface carries anything

  • A closing-line feed with at least a season of rows, so the market anchor can be weighted by measured out-of-sample log-loss instead of left blank.
  • Per-player prop lines from a source this project is allowed to use, plus a measured player–player correlation; until then no prop combination is priced at all.
  • An out-of-sample gain over the coin-flip baseline of at least 0.002 nats on the frozen thresholds — a number the current signals do not reach, which is why the leaderboard is empty.
