Decisions that need a human

  1. May we look at sportsbook prices at all? Not to copy them — the brief rules that out — but to compare. Without a market price there is no such thing as an edge, only self-consistency. Every strategy on the leaderboard today is graded against our own line, which is the honest thing to do but is not the same as being profitable.
  2. Do we want the leaderboard open to outside contributors? A competition with rewards implies yes, which implies a sandboxed runner. Option D1 on the improvements page.
  3. How should a "reward" be defined? Raw hit rate, return on a flat stake, or risk-adjusted return? The gates currently test hit rate only.
  4. What is the stake? Nothing is deployable on the current evidence, but the harness needs a staking rule before it can report anything a reviewer would act on.

Until the first question is answered, the most accurate description of this system is: a verified, self-consistent line-origination engine with an unproven strategy layer. That is a real achievement, and it is not yet a betting system.
