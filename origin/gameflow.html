Why rest is on this page and not in a strategy

Our rest-differential strategy (S3) put up 52.9% over 17 graded bets, p=1.000. That is indistinguishable from a coin, and we report it as such. Rest is a real effect in the literature, but with 17 qualifying games in this sample we have no business claiming it. It stays here as context for the game-flow read, not as a signal.

Home court

Our least-squares fit across 173 non-neutral games gives a home-court advantage of +1.59 points (two-sided p=0.104, df=172). At conventional significance thresholds that is not statistically distinguishable from zero. We use the fitted value in the line-origination model because it is the least-squares estimate, but a reviewer should know that in this particular 174-game sample the data does not establish home court at all. The Mexico City game (DEN vs IND, 7 Nov) is treated as neutral and is excluded from the fit.

Overtime

9 of 174 games went to overtime (5.2%). Overtime games average 252.1 combined points against 225.5 for regulation games — a gap of 26.7 points that matters for any total priced off regulation scoring rates. Our originated totals do not adjust for the possibility of overtime; they are regulation equivalents. That is a modelling choice, listed on the flagged page, not an oversight.
