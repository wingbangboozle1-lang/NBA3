Measured home-court advantage

111 home wins in 221 games with a home side = 50.2262% (away rate 49.7738%). The brief pins home-court advantage to the measured home-win rate instead of a fitted coefficient; this is that measurement, taken over every game this season that had a home side.

neutral-site games are excluded from both rates and are reported separately: the 2026-27 schedule has NBA Cup games at neutral venues, and the standings page prints a NEUTRAL column for them. Counting a neutral game as a home game would bias the rate the model pins HCA to.

The arithmetic is re-derivable from the table: sum the Home column for home wins, subtract the neutral-site games (a Cup game at a neutral site has no home team, and counting it as one would understate the rate), and divide.

Identity checks

  • Sum of every club's W = 222; sum of every club's L = 222 — equal, as it must be.
  • Home W-L 111-110; road W-L 110-111; neutral 1-1.
  • Home + road + neutral wins = 222 (total 222) — equal.
  • Home games 221 = road games 221 — equal.

On ordering: the page prints Western seeds 7-9 by wins rather than by winning percentage, so a club with a better percentage can sit below one with more wins and fewer games. Rows are published in the page's own order and the Seed column is that printed rank.
