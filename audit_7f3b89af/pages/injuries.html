Why this page exists

The canonical site publishes the 2026-11-14 04:15 AM ET report and states, on its own injuries page, that this is the newest report listed on the official index. That was true when it was read. It is not true now: the index lists reports through 2026-11-14 01:30 PM ET, and a probe of the 01:45 PM file returns AccessDenied, which is how the NBA's CDN answers for a report that does not exist.

The NBA's own rule, quoted from the index page: teams must file a game-day report “between 11 a.m. and 1 p.m. local time (and between 8 and 10 a.m. for tip-offs 5 p.m. or earlier)”. Every game tonight tips after 7 p.m. ET, so the 1 p.m. filings are the ones that govern these games — and the published snapshot predates all of them.
