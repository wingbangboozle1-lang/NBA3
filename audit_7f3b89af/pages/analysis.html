Does the originated line price rest?

A measured answer, with the sample size needed to settle it.

The question

Does our originated line already price rest, or does its error still carry a rest signal that a bettor could harvest?

An earlier session asked whether rest correlates with the actual margin. That is a fact about basketball. This asks whether rest correlates with the error of our own line, which is the question that decides whether a rest term belongs in the engine: if the line already prices rest, the correlation is zero and there is nothing to add.

DERIVED -- our arithmetic on official results, not an official NBA statistic.

Inputs: data/snapshots/games_2026-11-14.csv (183 official finals) and data/snapshots/canonical_2026-11-14.json -> oos_sample (140 out-of-sample games). Games used: 140.

Verdict

The originated line does not price rest, and the rest signal shows up in its error in the expected direction: +2.44 points of home margin per extra day of home rest (r = 0.142, 95% CI -0.025 to 0.301, permutation p = 0.097, BH-adjusted q = 0.244 over five tests). In the classic spot -- rested home team against a road team on a back-to-back -- the home side beat our line by 8.4 points on average across 14 games. Neither survives correction for testing five hypotheses at once, and the walk-forward test settles it: fitting the rest coefficient on earlier games only and applying it forward made the line WORSE by 0.33 points of RMSE (16.01 to 16.34), with the coefficient swinging between -1.1 and 16.1 points per day as the sample grew. Conclusion: do not add a rest term on this evidence. Re-test when the out-of-sample sample reaches roughly 389 games.

Method: Rest is days since that team's previous game in the official log. Error is actual home margin minus our predicted home margin. Permutation p-values are two-sided with 20000 shuffles, seed 20261114. Multiple comparisons across the five tests are controlled with Benjamini-Hochberg at q < 0.05.

What the walk-forward test actually did

For each game, in date order, the rest coefficient was re-fitted using only games already played, then applied to that game. Nothing from the future reached the coefficient, so this is something that could have been done in real time. It is the honest version of “would this have helped”.
