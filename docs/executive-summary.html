Executive summary

Data date 13 Nov 2026. What is established, what is not, and what must be decided before the project can produce reliable picks.

Bottom line

  • No strategy can be validated yet. Validation needs historical outcomes and market odds. Neither has a verified source in this repository.
  • No correlation values exist. Parlay and prop correlations need joint historical outcomes.
  • No picks are generated. The slate is captured (23 games on 11/13–11/15 plus 1 on 11/16), but there are no originated lines to price it.
  • The validation toolkit is in place. Binomial test, Wilson interval, Benjamini–Hochberg correction, parlay dependence ratio and a gate checker are implemented and tested (13 unit tests).
  • A legal question must be answered first. The Dutch regulator (Ksa) has stated that sports betting licences cover only bets on sports events or results, and that licence holders remain responsible for their offering. Whether this project's activity is covered is a legal question, not answered here.
