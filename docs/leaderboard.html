Reward rules (pre-registered)

  • Ranking metric: settled-pick ROI with Wilson 90% CI reported alongside; strategies with CI containing break-even are shown as 'not yet distinguished from luck'.
  • Minimum games before a strategy may be called 'skill-positive': strategy-specific gate N (30/20/15/25) AND CI excludes break-even.
  • Rewards: (1) weekly crown = highest settled ROI among gate-passing strategies; (2) 'Gate Champion' badge = first strategy to pass all gates; (3) unit allocation: top-ranked strategy receives 2x unit sizing in the following week's paper book, capped at 2%; others 1x.
  • Paper book starts at 100 units; bankruptcy rule: strategy dropped from live board below 50 units (historical record retained).
  • Every week the board freezes; no retroactive edits to settled picks.
  • PENDING/PROVISIONAL picks are never scored.
