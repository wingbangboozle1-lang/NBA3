# Methodology — NBA3 Line Origination

Status: **implemented and reproducible**. Code: `pipeline/run_model.py` (single file, no
external dependencies). All outputs are regenerated by the pipeline in `README.md`.

## 1. What we are originating
For every game within the next 3 days we publish:
- **Originated spread** (home margin expectation, from our model only)
- **Originated total** (expected combined score)
- **Fair moneylines** (from model win probability, no market input)
- **Win probabilities** per side

These are **our numbers**, not sourced facts. We never present them as market lines.

## 3. Models
**Model A — "prior"**: 2025-26 final adjusted NRtg difference between the teams, calibrated
to actual margin by OLS fit on an expanding window (minimum 30 games). Home edge included.

**Model B — "elo"**: in-season Elo with margin-of-victory multiplier (public FiveThirtyEight-
style formula `((|margin|+3)^0.8)/(7.5+0.006·Δelo)`), K=20, initialized from 2025-26 ratings
(1 rating point ≈ 15 Elo), home advantage 48 Elo (≈3.2 pts) applied consistently in both the
update and the prediction.

**Model C — "consensus"**: fixed 50/50 blend of A and B (chosen a priori; no tuning on the
test window). This is the headline model.

**Recalibration layer**: expanding-window OLS rescaling of C (`margin* = s·C + b`) plus a
single-parameter logistic win-probability fit — both estimated **only on data available
before each slate**.

**Totals**: shrunk (k=8) team offense/defense points-per-game, two-step opponent adjustment,
+1.5 home scoring edge (documented prior), expanding league-average total as baseline.

**Slate integrity**: predictions for all games on a date use only prior-date state (games in
a slate are simultaneous). No same-day leakage.

## 4. Validation gates (a strategy is only promoted if every gate passes)
| Gate | Threshold | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | ≥ 100 rated bets | Minimum sample to limit luck |
| G2 | Brier score ≤ 0.250 | Win-probability calibration |
| G3 | ROI > 0 at −110 (walk-forward) | Profitability under standard pricing |
| G4 | Permutation test p < 0.10 (2,000 shuffles) | Skill, not luck |
| G5 | Max drawdown ≤ 8 units | Risk control |
| G6 | Parlay leg correlation cap ≤ 0.5; same-team legs banned | Correlation risk |

**Backtest**: walk-forward over games 31–174 (138 rated games; burn-in 30). Pricing assumes
standard −110 because **no licensed odds feed is connected** — all ROI numbers are therefore
"vs our own line", not vs a market, and are labeled as such everywhere.

## 5. Results (as of 2026-11-13 data)
| Strategy | Rule | n | ROI | Permutation p | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| STRAT1 | Consensus spread side, \|pred\| ≥ 5, p ≥ 0.57/≤ 0.43 | 85 | +48.2% | < 0.0005 | **Provisional pass** (G1 short) |
| STRAT2 | ML in 0.60–0.78 band | 68 | +23.5% | 0.011 | **Provisional pass** (G1 short) |
| STRAT3 | Totals vs expanding league avg, gap ≥ 5 | 14 | −4.6% | 0.59 | **Not eligible** |
| STRAT4 | 2-leg parlays from STRAT1 legs (cross-game) | 15 | +14.6% | 0.29 | **Not eligible** |

Supporting metrics: consensus RMSE 14.95 (prior 15.36, elo 16.09); totals RMSE 19.3;
Brier 0.2001; naive-favorite baseline win rate 73.3% (n=105) vs STRAT1 77.6% — i.e., most of
STRAT1's raw win rate is this season's favorite-heavy results; the model adds lift and the
profit stream is unlikely to be luck (G4), but the sample is short.

**Read this carefully**: STRAT1/STRAT2 ROI is measured against our own originated line at an
assumed −110. A real market would already price much of this information; without an odds
feed we cannot measure closing-line value, so **market eligibility is unproven**. Treat the
"+48%" as model-skill evidence, not as a realistic money expectation.

## 6. Correlation analysis (for parlays)
| Measurement | Value | n |
|---|---|---|
| Same-game margin vs total | −0.018 | 138 |
| Consecutive-game team totals | +0.117 | ~1,150 team-games |
| STRAT1 vs STRAT3 daily P&L | +0.02 | shared days |
| Parlay example (p=0.58 legs, ρ=0.3 copula) | joint 0.374 vs independent 0.336 → fair price 2.67 vs 2.98 | — |

Practical rule published on the site: 2-leg parlays need a payout above the **independent**
fair price to compensate for undetected correlation; same-team/same-game legs are banned; the
measured same-game margin-total correlation this season is unusually near zero (noisy, n=138)
so we do not lean on it.

## 7. Injury handling (deliberately conservative)
We do **not** apply invented numeric injury adjustments (no player-level impact data is
available — see open questions). Games with Out/Doubtful/Questionable players on the official
report, or with a missing team submission, are flagged and their picks are marked "wait for
lineup confirmation".

## 8. Known limitations (flagged for review)
1. **No market odds feed** → no CLV, no market-relative validation (Q2).
2. **Sample short**: 138 rated games < G1's 100-bet threshold for two strategies; 2025-26
   game-level history would fix this (Q5).
3. **Totals model weak** (RMSE ≈ totals sd): no possessions/pace data (Q9).
4. **Props prototype unvalidated** (Q4).
5. Injury reports publish the day before (~5 PM ET): picks for days +2/+3 carry
   "report pending" flags (Q3).
6. This season shows unusual variance (margin sd 16.5, blowout rate 25.3%, home win 51.1%,
   home margin +1.6) — possibly small-sample noise; monitored.
