How a line is originated

home_pts = home_ORtg x (away_DRtg / 114.0) x (expected_pace / 100) away_pts = away_ORtg x (home_DRtg / 114.0) x (expected_pace / 100) margin_home = home_pts - away_pts + HCA # HCA = +1.51 pts, measured model_spread_home = -margin_home # book convention: negative = home favoured model_total = home_pts + away_pts P(home wins) = Phi(margin_home / 17.15)

Ratings are first shrunk toward league average by a games-played factor of G/(G+k) with k = 11.0, because a team 11 games into a season has not earned the right to be taken at face value. Phi is the normal CDF. Full derivation and the sensitivity of every number to the assumptions is on the method page.
