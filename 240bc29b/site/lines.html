Originated lines — our own model outputs (Derived, not economic)

We originate our own NBA lines (spread + total) for every game in the 2026-11-14 → 2026-11-17 window using a Massey least-squares margin model and a totals model, both shrunk, with shrinkage chosen by validating on the most recent 20% of past games only (never on the games being forecast). Each date is priced with the engine refitted on strictly earlier dates.

Not bets, not verified prices

Originated lines are Derived: they are our arithmetic on official results, not official NBA statistics, not market odds, and not betting advice. No licensed market-odds feed is connected, so no EV/ROI/CLV figure is an economic result. The full originated-line table with out-of-sample accuracy lives on the canonical dashboard (app/lines.html).
