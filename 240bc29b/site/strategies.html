Strategy leaderboard — validation gates, luck threshold, rewards

Strategy research treats line origination as a leaderboard competition: single bets, parlays and player-prop combos are proposed as experiments, each must pass validation gates, and correlation values are measured before any edge is claimed. Result: zero strategies are funded. No rule on the leaderboard clears the luck threshold, so nothing is a selection. Reward tiers are defined but not activated (owner decision — see Q-05).
