Method & rules

Line origination

Originated spreads and totals come from a Massey least-squares margin model (one rating per team plus a home-court term) and a totals model regressed on the two teams’ prior scoring environment. Both are shrunk (ridge penalty + blend toward the league mean), and both shrinkage parameters are chosen by validating on the most recent 20% of past games only — never on the games being forecast. Every date is priced with the engine refitted on strictly earlier dates; games are batched by date so a game cannot see another game tipped off the same night.

How luck is removed

Each strategy rule is measured out of sample against validation gates. A fair-line null simulation estimates how large an edge luck alone can produce; a rule must clear that luck threshold to be eligible. Mirror pairs and a null control (e.g. back the home team every game) confirm the gates are doing work.

Correlations

Correlations are measured from official results, not assumed: cross-game margin-residual and total-residual dependence, per-night hit-rate overdispersion. Measurable values are published; unmeasurable pairs are left blank.

Validation gates (proposed, not approved)

G1 sample size · G2 statistical significance · G3 edge vs luck threshold · G4 stability · G5 correlation awareness · G6 injury/rotation gating · G7 no data leakage · G8 economic pricing (blocked without odds feed) · G9 compliance.

Legal boundary

NBA.com Terms of Use §9 limits use of NBA statistics in connection with gambling. No licensed market-odds feed is connected, so no EV/ROI/CLV figure here is an economic result. This is research, not betting advice.
