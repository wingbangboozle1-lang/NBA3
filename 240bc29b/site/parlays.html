Parlay & prop combos — framework, not priced

Single bets, parlays (same-game and cross-game) and player-prop combos are the three combo types in scope. Correlations are measured from official results rather than assumed: cross-game margin-residual correlation and total-residual correlation are computed, and neither rejects independence in the current sample, so parlay dependence is not priced as an edge.
