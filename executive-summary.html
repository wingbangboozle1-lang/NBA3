4 · Validation gates and luck removal (what "skill-based" means here)

GatePurpose (luck it removes)
G1 Provenance — every input official or derivedBlocks unsourced numbers entering a signal
G2 No lookahead (walk-forward only)Blocks hindsight bias
G3 Minimum sample n ≥ 100Blocks small-sample flukes
G4 Power / observed-effect sample checkBlocks under-powered "signals"
G5 Raw p < 0.05 (exact binomial)Blocks chance-level hit rates
G6 Survives Benjamini–Hochberg FDR (α=0.05)Blocks multiple-testing luck (testing 8+ strategies inflates false positives)
G7 Split-half direction stabilityBlocks first-half-only luck
Permutation test (2,000 shuffles, edge/)Blocks ROI that shuffling the schedule reproduces
Luck-adjusted leaderboard score (lab/)Multiplies skill by (1 − luck-p) so a lucky run cannot top the board (measured p=0.4294 → treated as pure luck)

All measured same-game intervals include zero: same-game correlations in this sample are statistically indistinguishable from independence, which means same-game parlay pricing cannot yet be adjusted for dependence with confidence. The edge/ engine's parlay example is published with the corrected arithmetic (defect FX-01 fixed 2026-11-14 — the earlier value of 0.58 came from an integration loop that ignored the first-leg threshold): for two 0.58 legs at an assumed ρ=+0.3 the corrected joint probability is 0.3832 (fair decimal 2.61), which is ABOVE the independent-product 0.3364 (fair 2.97) — for positive correlation the joint win probability rises and the fair parlay odds shorten; the joint probability is bounded by [0.16, 0.58] and reaches 0.58 only at ρ=1. The correction was checked three ways (closed form at ρ=0, adaptive Simpson, 200k-draw Monte Carlo). One same-day parlay combo is published, with legs from different games to avoid same-game dependence (correlation_report.json · corrections page).
