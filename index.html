NBA Edge Lab — canonical dashboard

Official-source research dashboard. Every table row links to its source; anything that could not be confirmed is left blank and flagged.

We originate our own NBA lines, then try to prove they are worth anything.

One dashboard for the whole pipeline: lines originated for today and the next three days, 15 strategy rules measured out of sample against nine validation gates and ranked on a leaderboard with an explicit luck threshold, the correlations that were actually measured (and the ones left blank), the official injury report with the rotation minutes it vacates, game-flow factors, an executive summary, the open questions, improvement suggestions awaiting a decision, and a review page for everything still unverified.
