Executive summary

Important: the repository now contains several independent model/data products, not one reconciled system. Some sub-sites publish originated lines, candidate picks, and statistical summaries. Their inputs, date windows, model outputs, and eligibility labels differ. The passing local build/test checks establish software consistency only; they do not establish that the underlying NBA values are official, licensed, current, or predictive. Do not treat any displayed pick, edge, fair price, ROI, correlation, or injury impact as an actionable or verified result until the items on Flagged for Review are resolved.

The best-supported outcome of this audit is a decision-support plan: secure and document permissible data access; select one versioned source of truth; log pre-game forecasts and snapshots; run time-ordered validation and a prospective paper trial; and only then enable a leaderboard or picks. Individual game outcomes remain noisy—validation can quantify uncertainty, not remove luck.

Current site review date: 14 November 2026 (UTC). Fixed snapshots in the repository are not live feeds and may become stale.

How to reduce luck’s influence

  • Freeze a model and record each forecast, information cutoff, price, and data snapshot before tip-off.
  • Use chronological, leakage-resistant walk-forward tests and a prospective paper phase; do not select thresholds after viewing results.
  • Report probability calibration and proper scores as well as uncertainty intervals, sample sizes, drawdowns, and net returns after documented costs.
  • Estimate combo/joint probabilities directly; measure dependence on matched samples and disclose uncertainty. Never assume parlay legs are independent without evidence.
  • Rank strategies only after common gates and a shared settlement/pricing specification have been applied.
