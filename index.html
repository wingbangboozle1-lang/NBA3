De-biasing “luck”

Luck cannot be removed from individual outcomes. Reduce its influence in evaluation: proper scoring rules, calibration plots, large pre-registered out-of-sample samples, confidence intervals or bootstrap by game/date, fixed settlement rules, no cherry-picking, and record every forecast before tip-off. Separate model skill from variance, vig, limits, and execution. A positive short-run ROI is not proof of edge.

Injuries, rotations & game flow

The NBA publishes official injury-report files; each report is a timestamped status snapshot, not a playing-time forecast or medical clearance. Status can change. Use the newest available report and preserve prior versions; avoid converting “questionable” or “available” into a minutes number.
