What this is, in one paragraph

Lines are originated from a model fitted on 174 verified NBA.com results and validated walk-forward on 114 out-of-sample games. On spread the model beats a constant baseline by 7.5% (paired p=0.0170); on totals the gain is 2.0% and is not significant. No strategy yet clears the eligibility gates, no market price exists in the repository to measure profit against, and player props remain unbuildable without full minutes data. Those three limits are the reason the site shows model output rather than bets, and they are documented row by row with sources.
