Read the projections this way. Where the two estimators agree closely the line is marked high confidence; where they disagree by more than four points it is low, and that disagreement — not the blended number — is the signal. On this slate the two estimators disagree by more than 12 points on 10 of 25 games, which is the honest measure of how little a 22-game log supports.
