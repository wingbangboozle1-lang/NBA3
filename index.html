Executive summary

NBA3 originates its own point spreads and totals for every NBA game, then tests strategies against those lines rather than against a sportsbook's. Every input is read from an official NBA source; every derived number is reproducible from model/build.py.

  1. The data is complete and reconciles exactly. 174 games from 2026-10-20 to 2026-11-12, 348 team-games, matching the official games-played column on nba.com/stats/teams/advanced for all 30 teams with zero discrepancy. Per-team reconciliation is on the Data & Sources page.
  2. Our originated lines beat a naive baseline on the side, but not on the total. Walk-forward over 114 out-of-sample games, the blend model cuts margin RMSE from 17.30 to 16.18 points (6.5%). For totals the best model we tested is worse than simply guessing the league average (19.56 vs 20.35), so we do not claim any skill on totals and we say so in the numbers.
  3. No strategy clears every validation gate. 0 of 8 pass. Two are provisionally interesting — fading our own totals of 235+ (21/24 unders, p=0.0003) and backing road favourites of 8+ (20/28, p=0.0357) — but both sit far below the 100-bet sample floor the gate requires, and both were found in a search over eight candidates, so the honest reading is under-powered, not disproven.
  4. The parlay correlation we can measure is small. The residual correlation between our own spread and our own total is r=+0.091 (95% CI [-0.09, +0.27], n=114). The confidence interval includes zero, so we cannot reject independence — but we also cannot assert it. Cross-game parlay legs were not measured at all and remain an assumption.
  5. Injuries move minutes, and we quantify what we can. The official NBA injury report for 13-14 Nov lists 85 player-rows marked Out or Doubtful. Where the player appears in the official minutes table we convert his status into minutes at risk; where he does not, we leave the impact blank and flag it rather than guessing.
  6. Home court this season is small and not statistically significant. Our least-squares estimate is +1.59 points (p=0.104, df=172). We still use it in the model because it is the fitted value, but a reviewer should know the data does not establish it.

Bottom line: the pipeline is built, sourced and reproducible, and it produces defensible originated lines for every game in the next three days. It has not yet produced a strategy that survives its own validation gates. Deploying any of the provisional signals with real money today would be betting on a small sample, not on demonstrated skill.
