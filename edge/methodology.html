Methodology

Full document: docs/methodology.md. Everything is reproducible via pipeline/.

What we originate

For every game within the next 3 days: spread (expected home margin), total, fair moneylines, win probabilities. These are our numbers, never presented as market lines or sourced facts.

Models

  • Model A (prior): 2025-26 final adjusted NRtg difference, calibrated to actual margin by OLS on an expanding window (min 30 games). Home edge included.
  • Model B (elo): in-season Elo with margin-of-victory multiplier ((|margin|+3)^0.8)/(7.5+0.006·Δelo), K=20, initialized from 2025-26 ratings (1 pt ≈ 15 Elo), home advantage 48 Elo (≈3.2 pts) in both update and prediction.
  • Model C (consensus): fixed 50/50 blend — chosen a priori, no tuning on the test window.
  • Recalibration: expanding-window OLS rescale of C + single-parameter logistic win-probability fit, past-only at each step (no leakage).
  • Totals: shrunk (k=8) opponent-adjusted team scoring, +1.5 home edge prior, expanding league-average baseline.
  • Slate integrity: predictions for a date use only prior-date state (games are simultaneous).

Known limitations

  1. No market odds feed → no CLV / market-relative validation (Q2).
  2. Short sample: 138 rated games; 2025-26 history would fix G1 (Q5).
  3. Totals model weak without pace data (Q9).
  4. Props prototype unvalidated (Q4).
  5. Injury reports publish day-before; +2/+3-day picks carry pending flags (Q3).
  6. Season variance is unusual (blowouts 25.3%, home win 51.1%); monitored.
