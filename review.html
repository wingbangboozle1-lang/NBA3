Flagged for review

Everything this build could not verify, could not measure, or deliberately chose not to assert. These items do not block publication — they block any claim that the system is validated, and they are listed here so a reviewer can act on them after the site is live.

Nothing on this list is hidden elsewhere. Where a value could not be confirmed from a source it is blank in the tables and marked unverified. No value anywhere on this site was inferred, interpolated, or filled from a plausible-looking default.

IR-04Home-court edge and distribution widths are partly assumedThe home edge is measured (+1.51 pts) but its 95% CI spans zero. The shrinkage constant k is assumed, not fitted. Every line moves one-for-one with these. Thin sample: 86 games, one 12-day window. source
IR-05Team ratings come from a secondary sourceORtg, DRtg and pace are Basketball-Reference figures, not an NBA-published export. They are reliable and widely used, but they are not the league's own numbers and are labelled secondary throughout. No machine-readable official team-rating endpoint was reachable in this session. source
IR-06Market prices are one book, one snapshotAll prices come from a single provider relayed by ESPN. 'Edge' therefore means edge against that provider at that minute. No market consensus was captured. A second and third feed are required to make a market-level claim. source

One lookup per athlete; the game exposes 1,170 price points. source

  2. IR-08 — get a minutes source. It reopens props, the biggest market.
  3. IR-15 — settle the legal question before anything is published as actionable.

What is not on this list

These were checked and are clean, so they are recorded as verified rather than flagged:

  • All 30 teams' net ratings equal ORtg minus DRtg, and every team's SRS equals MOV plus SOS — the arithmetic in the ratings table is internally consistent.
  • All 86 transcribed game results reconcile with the season win-loss records; no team's subset wins or losses exceed its season total.
  • All 9 market snapshots agree with the favourite and line stated in the provider's own detail string.
  • Every game ID on the slate resolves between the league's schedule and the market feed, and every box score in the completed-games sample links to a dated box score.
  • Every table row on this site links to an http source, and the build fails if one does not.
