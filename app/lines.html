Originated lines

Lines originated by this project for every game of the 2026-11-14 → 2026-11-17 window. They are produced by the walk-forward engine described on the method page and are not market prices: no licensed odds feed is connected, so there is nothing to compare them with and no claim of value is made anywhere on this page.

How to read the spread column. The spread is quoted for the home team, the way a book prints it: a negative number means the home team is favoured by that many points. The fair moneyline is our own price with no vig. Because these are our own lines, the probability of covering them is 50% by construction — see the prices page for why that matters.

Read the totals with care. Out of sample our totals model is worse than a constant league-average total (RMSE 19.45 vs 19.39) and runs 1.5 points hot. The published totals are shrunk toward the league mean. See NF-49.

Spread is quoted for the home team. Margin range is the model margin ± 1.96 × out-of-sample residual sd. Pace is the mean of the two teams’ official PACE.
