RQ02 Originate own NBA lines Models Done Lines originated for all 27 games of the live window (re-originated 2026-11-14 on 183 games: all 27 lines moved, median 1.1 points, max 4.9) by a walk-forward Massey margin model with ridge shrinkage plus a totals model shrunk toward the league mean. Both shrinkage parameters are chosen by validating on the most recent 20 percent of past games only. Code: scripts/strategy_lab.py, seeded and deterministic. The margin model is only modestly better than a constant baseline and the totals model is worse than one. Both facts are published rather than hidden. See NF-49. 140 out-of-sample forecasts; margin RMSE 15.945 vs 16.921 baseline, total RMSE 19.449 vs 19.386 baseline https://www.nba.com/games?date=

RQ08 Remove luck; build selectively skill-based strategies Validation Method applied; no skill demonstrated Exact binomial tests against the market's fair-line rate; Benjamini-Hochberg across the board; a 5,000-iteration whole-board Monte Carlo producing a 28.9 pp luck threshold; date-block bootstrap for return intervals; split-half stability; mirror pairs so the losing side is visible; and a null control that must fail. It does: 48.6 percent on 140 games, p=0.80 (recomputed on the 183-game log, 2026-11-14). The methods detect luck; they cannot manufacture skill from 183 games. Nothing on the board beats the threshold. See NF-51. Null control ST-NUL fails 8 of 9 gates, which is the check that the gates work https://www.nba.com/games?date=
