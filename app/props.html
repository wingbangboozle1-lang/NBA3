Player props

No prop on this page is a bet, and none carries a price. Two things are missing and neither can be worked around. First, no licensed prop prices are connected, so there is no line to compare a projection with. Second, this repository holds season averages only — no per-player game logs, so the scoring distribution cannot be estimated and an over/under probability cannot be computed. The market-line and probability columns are therefore blank, and blank is the truthful value.

What the page can show honestly: the official season averages of the highest-minute players on each team in the window, and our projection, which for now is simply that average with no adjustment. Both are labelled for what they are.

MIN, PTS, REB and AST are official season averages. The projection repeats them unchanged because no minutes, usage or matchup adjustment can be verified without game logs.

Prop combinations

All four combinations the brief asks for are blocked, for the same reason: the joint distribution of two player markets cannot be estimated from season averages alone. They are listed with what would unblock them on the parlays page.

What it would take to make this page useful

  1. Capture per-player game logs from the official players table by date — about 4,000 rows a season. Owner decision: CQ-39.
  2. Fit a distribution per player per market, with minutes as the exposure variable.
  3. Connect a licensed prop price feed so a comparison is possible at all. Owner decision: Q-03, tracked on the questions page.
  4. Measure the within-game correlation between a player's markets, then price the combinations. Until that measurement exists, any joint probability would be invented.
