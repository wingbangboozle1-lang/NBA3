1. What this project is

A research pipeline that originates its own NBA lines, tests them out of sample against validation gates, and publishes every intermediate number with the official source it came from. It is deliberately conservative: a number that cannot be confirmed is left blank and listed on the Flagged for review page rather than estimated.

4. How luck is kept out of the score

Luck cannot be removed from a single game; it can only be kept out of the evaluation. The rules applied here are: exact binomial tests instead of eyeballing win rates; a Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery adjustment when several strategies are tested at once; a split-half check that the effect points the same way in both halves of the sample; a minimum-sample gate (n ≥ 100 out-of-sample games) that no strategy currently meets; and walk-forward evaluation, so a game is never scored with information that did not exist at its tip-off. Short-run profitability is not treated as evidence of skill — only out-of-sample calibration and uncertainty-adjusted results count.

5. What a reader must not do with this site

  • Do not treat the derived lines as official NBA values. They are this project's model output; the source column links to the method and to the official inputs it used.
  • Do not treat the picks as betting advice. No strategy has passed the full gate set, no licensed price feed is connected, and the legal questions on the Open questions page are unanswered.
  • Do not compare a status from one injury report with a status from another. The NBA updates the report every 15 minutes; only the newest published snapshot is used here and the difference against the previous one is listed on the Injuries & rotation page.
