Overview

Method & rules

1. What is originated here

Two numbers per game: a point spread (the home team's expected margin) and a total (expected combined points, regulation-equivalent). Both come from a walk-forward model whose inputs are official: the official schedule, official team advanced statistics, official final scores and the official injury report. Nothing is copied from a sportsbook.

2. How the model is tested

  • Walk-forward. The first 60 captured games are a burn-in; every later game is scored with a model fitted only on games that had already happened.
  • Baselines. Each model is compared with a naive league-average forecast. The margin model beats its baseline (RMSE 16.18 vs 17.30); the total model does not (20.35 vs 19.56) and is reported as such.
  • Exact binomial tests. Win rates are tested against 50% with an exact binomial test rather than a normal approximation.
  • Multiple testing. When several strategies are tested, Benjamini–Hochberg controls the false-discovery rate at 5%.
  • Split-half stability. A strategy must point the same way in the first and the second half of the sample.
  • Minimum sample. n ≥ 100 out-of-sample games before a strategy can be eligible. No strategy reaches it on the current sample.

4. Removing luck from the evaluation

Luck cannot be removed from an individual game result. It can be kept out of the score by: scoring strategies out of sample; adjusting for the number of strategies tested; reporting confidence intervals rather than point estimates; requiring a direction that is stable across halves of the sample; refusing to score a strategy on fewer than 100 out-of-sample games; and never re-using a result to tune the rule that produced it. A positive short-run return is not evidence of skill, and none of the strategies on this site has demonstrated skill yet.

5. Injuries and rotation

The official injury report publishes a participation status and a reason. It does not publish minutes, expected minutes or production impact, and a status is not a medical clearance. The pipeline therefore (a) captures the newest report with its timestamp, (b) keeps the previous snapshots so status changes can be shown, (c) converts an OUT into a minutes vacuum only when the player's official minutes-per-game are captured, and (d) leaves the vacuum blank and flagged when they are not. Status-to-availability weights (Out 0.00, Doubtful 0.25, Questionable 0.60, Probable 0.90, Available 1.00) are this project's assumption and are flagged as such.

6. Legal and data-use boundary

  • The NBA Terms of Use restrict the use of NBA Statistics in connection with gambling and restrict reproduction. This is a research repository, publishes no market prices and no betting advice, and links to every official source it uses. The review points at nba.com/termsofuse are listed on the Flagged for review page.
  • Publishing betting-related functionality or recommendations in the Netherlands requires a licence under the Betting and Gaming Act and a Ksa licence for online sports-betting offers; the statutory position is summarised on the Open questions page.
  • No licensed price feed is connected, so no profit, ROI or market comparison can be published here. Every price shown is our own fair price.
