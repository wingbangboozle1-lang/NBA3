Pre-registration

Every strategy is pre-registered before its first bet: signal, thresholds, universe and an exact settlement rule, frozen with a sha256 in data/app/prereg/. Frozen files are never overwritten — python3 scripts/preregister.py freeze refuses to touch an existing file, and verify recomputes every hash. 15 strategies are frozen. No money bet has been placed (no rule is funded), so every file here predates the first bet by construction.
