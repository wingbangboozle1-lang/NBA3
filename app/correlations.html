What the correlation numbers mean for combinations

  • Same-game parlay (side + total). Measured joint hit rate 0.246 against 0.219 if the legs were independent. The difference is inside the noise of a 114-game sample, so no dependence premium is claimed either way.
  • Player-prop combinations. Not measurable without per-player game logs: the three player-level rows above are blank by policy.
  • Cross-game parlays. Independence is an assumption, not a result. It is priced as an assumption and flagged.
