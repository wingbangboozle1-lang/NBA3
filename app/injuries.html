3. What the absences do to a rotation

The table below sums the official season minutes — and the official season points, rebounds and assists — of every player listed Out or Doubtful for a team. It is a floor, not an estimate: the captured statistics table is the league's top 31 players by minutes, so an injured player outside that table contributes nothing to the sum, and the count of such players is shown alongside. See NF-52.

Read the zeros carefully — this column changed on 2026-11-14. Until this round the roll-up compared the injury report's Lastname, Firstname against the statistics table's Firstname Lastname with a rule that could never match, so every row published 0.0 or blank and the feature was dead behind a table that looked correct. The matching is fixed (sorted name tokens, generational suffixes dropped, and the team code must agree before a match is credited — flag F-DF3-03), which is why rows can now show a real vacuum. What is left is an input-coverage limit, stated as a number: 1 of 9 filed team-sides on this slate has a quantifiable vacuum, because most players listed Out or Doubtful tonight sit outside a top-31 minutes table. The fix is to capture a rotation-depth table, which is CS-48.

Who absorbs those minutes, and how much of the vacated production they replace, is not quantified anywhere on this site. Answering that needs per-player game logs and on/off splits, which this repository does not have. The vacuum is published; the replacement is deliberately blank. Publishing a redistribution assumption as if it were a measurement is exactly the kind of number that gets a bankroll staked on it.

“Minutes vacated” is the sum of official season minutes-per-game over players listed Out. “Unquantified players” is how many of them are outside the captured minutes table and therefore contribute zero to the sum.
