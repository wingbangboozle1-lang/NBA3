2. What the absences do to a rotation

The table below sums the official season minutes — and the official season points, rebounds and assists — of every player listed Out for a team. It is a floor, not an estimate: only the top 31 players by minutes were captured, so an injured player outside that table contributes nothing to the sum, and the count of such players is shown alongside. See NF-52.

Who absorbs those minutes, and how much of the vacated production they replace, is not quantified anywhere on this site. Answering that needs per-player game logs and on/off splits, which this repository does not have. The vacuum is published; the replacement is deliberately blank. Publishing a redistribution assumption as if it were a measurement is exactly the kind of number that gets a bankroll staked on it.

“Minutes vacated” is the sum of official season minutes-per-game over players listed Out. “Unquantified players” is how many of them are outside the captured minutes table and therefore contribute zero to the sum.
