Refresh & provenance gates

The scheduled refresh job (.github/workflows/refresh.yml, cron four times a day around the official injury-report filing windows) fetches the official injury index and newest report, the schedule and results pages, every finished game's official game book, and the three official team-stat pages. The site rebuild runs only after the freshness and provenance gates below pass; on gate failure the run exits non-zero and the site keeps the last good build.

What the refresh stores

  • the newest official injury report PDF (sha256 + retrieval stamp) and the consecutive-report diff against the previous capture;
  • the schedule window today..today+3 with game ids and tip times;
  • per-game results: date, teams, final score, quarter scores — settled only from verified finals (see the settlement page);
  • per-player game logs (minutes, points, rebounds, assists, opponent) from every official game book captured;
  • the three team-stat pages (traditional, opponent, advanced), re-read and re-cut into the model inputs;
  • top-8 minutes shares and replacement shares for the minutes-redistribution model.
