Every game in the official per-game results feed: date, teams, final score and quarter scores, settled only from verified official finals. 23 of 23 rows are settled. A row settles only when the game state is Final and the quarter scores sum to the printed final for both teams (checked offline in capture_official_2026_11_15.py and results_feed.py). A row is settled only after the final score is verified from official reads (at minimum the official scoreboard; 2026-11-15 rows carry a second independent read from the game summary page). When quarter scores are captured they must sum to the printed final for both sides or the row is withheld and flagged; quarter values that conflict between two official documents are published from the game-page LINESCORES with the conflict recorded on the row and flagged for review, never repaired by hand. Rows without captured quarter scores are settled on the verified final alone, with that noted in the settlement stamp.
