Two-estimator projections

Headline: the two estimators disagree by 7.1 points on average across the window. That disagreement is the confidence statement — a small disagreement means both independent methods land in the same place, a large one means the game is genuinely hard to price. The equal-weight blend of the two is published underneath as a secondary line only; it is never the headline and never the only number shown.

Estimator A is a ridge margin model (Massey-style team strengths) fit on the official completed-game log. Estimator B is a possession-efficiency model from the three official team-stat pages captured 2026-11-15: expected points are possessions x points-per-possession. Home-court advantage is pinned to the measured home-win rate (0.5266 over 188 official games → 1.13 points) and is never a free fit coefficient.

3. Market-anchored prior

Blended lines are blank and flagged. blank + unverified: no closing-line feed is connected, so no blended line may be published Feed status: not connected.

When a closing-line feed is connected, the published line becomes w x model + (1-w) x closing with w from the measured out-of-sample log losses (blended = w * model + (1 - w) * closing, w = exp(-logloss_model) / (exp(-logloss_model) + exp(-logloss_closing)), log losses measured out-of-sample). Until then only the model line exists and it is labelled as such. The placeholder feed file is https://github.com/wingbangboozle1-lang/NBA3/blob/main/data/app/closing_lines.json.
