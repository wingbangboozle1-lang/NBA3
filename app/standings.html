Standings & records

The brief asks the scheduled job to refresh the official standings for the rolling window alongside the schedule, team statistics and injury report. The job fetches www.nba.com/standings on every run and stores the page verbatim in its capture directory (see scripts/refresh_official.py step 3a), promoting only what the fetched HTML actually prints.

Standing caveat: the official standings page renders its table client-side, so the fetched HTML carries no parsable W-L rows — the promotion records 0 rows from the standings page and the values are published as not captured rather than being derived. The record cells the official game pages and game books print are shown below with their own provenance instead. Standings position, games back and win pct are blank for the same reason: no captured official source prints them here.
