CR-34 Model Defects found and fixed in the strategy engine during this build Four defects were found by reading the engine's own output rather than trusting it: (1) two-leg parlays were being scored against a 50% fair-line rate instead of 25%, which put a losing same-game parlay at the top of the first draft of the leaderboard; (2) the permutation test shuffled win flags within a date, which cannot change the total and so returned p=1.000 for every rule — it was replaced with a whole-board Monte Carlo against the fair-line null; (3) the 'high-octane' threshold used the median of the whole sample's projections, which is look-ahead — it now uses only prior projections; (4) an under-regularised Massey model printed a 28-point spread, so ridge and totals shrinkage are now chosen by validating on the most recent 20% of past games. Irregularity — found and fixed in this session Keep publishing defects found this way. A leaderboard whose top row is a bug is worse than no leaderboard. 2026-11-14 https://github.com/wingbangboozle1-lang/NBA3/blob/main/scripts/strategy_lab.py
