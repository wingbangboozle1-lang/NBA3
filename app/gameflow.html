What is deliberately missing from this page

Missing factor Why it is not here
Possession-level pace and efficiency for the window The official team advanced page publishes season figures, not a per-game projection. The pace column is the mean of two season figures, and it is labelled as such.schedule
An overtime model 9 of 174 games went to overtime. That is one rate, not a model. Applying a flat 5.17% to every game is the honest ceiling of what this data supports.schedule
Referee, travel and altitude effects No official source for these was captured, so they are absent rather than estimated.schedule
Live in-game win probability Requires play-by-play, which this repository does not have.schedule
