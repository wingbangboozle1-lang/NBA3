Prices for the window

0 of 15 rules on the board are funded and 0 are provisional, so on the evidence in this repository no game in this window is a selection. What follows is our own price for every game in the window, published so that the day a rule clears its gates the call can be checked against it. Ranked board: strategy leaderboard; the threshold none of them clears: validation gates.

This page does not contain bets. Every price below is our own fair price against our own originated line, which makes its expected value exactly zero. No licensed odds feed is connected to this repository, so there is no market line to beat and no expected value can be stated. Read this page as “here is what we think each game is worth”, not as “here is what to bet”. Suggestion CS-36 proposes renaming it to match.

One row for every one of the 34 games in the 2026-11-15 → 2026-11-18 window. The brief asks for picks up to three days ahead; this is today plus the maximum three-day horizon, generated from our originated lines. Games on 2026-11-13 were live on the official schedule page when it was read and are excluded — see NF-54.

“Rotation availability” is the explicit minutes-redistribution output: the top-8 minutes share remaining after every player designated OUT on the official injury report is removed and their share redistributed proportionally (replacement shares per player on the injuries page; sources: official game books and the official per-player minutes snapshot). “Model side” names the team our line favours, printed the way a book would print it. “Availability” is the four-state verdict from the official filing rule — see the Report-availability page. Only REPORTED means the absence counts in the lines table are real; everywhere else they are blank, not zero. “Re-price after” is the official filing deadline of whichever side has not filed, printed on that arena’s local clock: no time-zone conversion is asserted anywhere in this repository, so the deadline is not directly comparable with the ET tip time in the second column (CQ-43).
