What this surface is, and what it is not

This is the gated surface. A pick appears here only after (1) its strategy was frozen in writing before its first bet, (2) the pick was sealed with a sha256 hash before tip-off, and (3) it passed the validation gates. It is graded only from an official final that two independent reads agree on.

No pick on this page is a recommendation, a stake, or advice. There is no market price in this repository, so no edge can be claimed against one. The picks are originated numbers being graded, and the grading is the point.

The competiton: strategies as a leaderboard

Strategies compete on graded results, not on claimed ability. A strategy stays on the board only while it passes the validation gates; anything leaning on a team outside the luck noise band is refused publication, and none currently clears that band.
