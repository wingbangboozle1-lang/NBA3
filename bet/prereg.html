Pre-registration

Three strategies, frozen in writing before their first bet, with the settlement rule spelled out to the push.

A pre-registration file is written once, before the strategy's first bet, and cannot be edited afterwards: scripts/ledger.py refuses to overwrite one and refuses to seal a pick whose strategy has no frozen file. Each file names the signal, the thresholds, the universe and the exact settlement rule.
