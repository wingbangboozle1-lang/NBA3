Why 11 picks and not more

Three strategies are frozen; two produced eligible games across 3 days (2026-11-15, 2026-11-16, 2026-11-17). The brief asks for picks up to three days ahead, so the publisher seals the whole priced window rather than the next day only — a pick sealed two days early is a stronger test of the pre-registration than one sealed on the morning of the game.

  • S2 (disagreement) — 9 picks. The band is 5–20 pp with both estimators on the same side of the moneyline; games outside the band, or where the estimators disagree on the side, are skipped and logged as a failed G3.
  • S1 (home back-to-back fade) — 2 picks. Eligible only when the home team is on the second night of a back-to-back and the road team is not.
  • S3 (totals) — 0 picks. No rule has passed validation, so it publishes nothing rather than a provisional pick.
A defect this page caught in its own publisher. The first version derived back-to-backs from the model's game sample, which ends before the slate — so it reported zero back-to-backs and S1 sealed nothing. It also sealed only the first slate day. Both are fixed: the schedule of record comes from scripts/rest_travel.py (three unioned official captures) and the publisher covers the full three-day window.
