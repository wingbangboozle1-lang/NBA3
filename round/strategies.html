Strategy board: 25 candidates, 0 published

A strategy is published only if it clears every gate. This page shows the gate, the result, and — for every blocked row — the specific reason it is blocked.

Nothing is published, and the reason is arithmetic. A strategy may not lean on a team whose record is outside a ±2 z-score band. A z-score needs games; no team has more than two in the captured log, and this build requires four before it will treat a z as meaningful. So no team can be shown inside the band, so no strategy clears the gate. The honest state of the board is "insufficient evidence", and it is printed rather than papered over with a small-sample favourite.

The gates a strategy must clear

  1. Two estimators agree. No line without both A and B.
  2. The team is inside the luck band. |z| ≤ 2, on at least four captured games.
  3. A frozen pre-registration exists. Signal, thresholds, universe and the exact settlement rule, hashed before the first bet.
  4. The pick is sealed before tip. Its hash is published ahead of the game.
  5. Settlement uses an official final only. The game book, not a summary.

What would unblock this

  • More settled games. Four per team is the floor this build sets; the log has 22 finals and needs roughly 150 before every team clears it.
  • A licensed odds feed. Without a market price there is no benchmark to measure a strategy's out-of-sample log-loss against, so "skill" cannot be separated from "the model likes a team".
