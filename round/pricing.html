The naive product assumes independence. The joint uses a bivariate normal copula at the measured ρ; with ρ = 0 it collapses back to the product, which is asserted by a unit test.

What is withheld, and why

  • Player-prop combos. No measured ρ, so no price. The legs and the reason are recorded; the number is not.
  • A closing-line comparison. No feed. The model's price is printed alone rather than against an invented market.
