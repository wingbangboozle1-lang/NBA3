S01NavigationRetire the older deployment trees (app/ legacy, v2/, projection/, bet/, dashboard/, docs/, edge/, dash/, hub/, site/, origin/, verify/, lab/, research/, final/, and the seven audit trees) from the Pages deployment and serve only this dashboard plus a provenance archive page.There are 20+ parallel page trees from earlier sessions, each with its own numbers. S03Data captureBackfill the 2026-27 season to date (roughly 120 game days) by reading each date's official scoreboard and its game books, into a single append-only results file.Every blocked strategy is blocked on sample size, not on method. The gates, the luck band, the walk-forward benchmark and the independence test (Q11) are all written and waiting for data they cannot currently get. This is the single highest-value change on this page.Lawaiting your decisionwww.nba.com
S04MarketsAdd a closing-line feed and switch the market-anchored prior on.The weight is already written as exp(-LL_model)/(exp(-LL_model)+exp(-LL_closing)) and the field is null. With a feed, every line gains a second opinion and the site can finally say whether the model or the market is better - which is the entire point of originating lines.Mawaiting your decisionwww.nba.com
S05PresentationReplace the per-game disagreement column with a distribution view: a small histogram of |A-B| across the window, with each game's position in it.Reading 25 raw deltas requires the reader to hold the distribution in their head. The same information as a position within a distribution is read in one glance and makes an outlier game obviously an outlier.Sawaiting your decisionwww.nba.com
S06PresentationShow a compact sparkline of each team's measured margin over the captured log next to its rating, with a marker for the estimated rating and a band for its uncertainty.A rating without a sense of its own uncertainty invites false confidence. Two games per team produce a rating with a very wide interval, and the picture would say that faster than any table.Sawaiting your decisionwww.nba.com
S07VerificationAdd a settlement self-check that re-reads the official game book a fixed interval after settlement and raises a flag if the printed value changed.Settlement currently records a stamp. If the league ever corrects a game book, this site would keep the old number with an old stamp and no warning. A delayed re-read makes a silent correction visible.Sawaiting your decisionwww.nba.com
S08AccessibilityAdd a printable one-page summary that carries the slate, the two estimators' disagreement, every flagged row and the settlement state on a single sheet.The dashboard is built for a screen.
