The two estimators, and where they disagree

One blended rating presented as a single truth is the failure mode this project is built to avoid. There are two estimators, they share no input, and the gap between them is the confidence number the site leads with.

Why these two, and why they are independent

A · result-based

Ridge-regularised least squares on final margins: one strength per team, with the home-court term pinned rather than free. Inputs are final margins and nothing else. It knows nothing about shooting, pace or schedule, which is exactly why it can be checked against B.

B · efficiency-based

Expected margin from possessions and defensive/offensive rating: (OffRtg_home − DefRtg_away) × poss/100 − (OffRtg_away − DefRtg_home) × poss/100, with the same pinned home-court term. It never looks at a final score.

If both read the same rating they would agree by construction and the disagreement would always be zero. That is the trap; these two do not share an input.
