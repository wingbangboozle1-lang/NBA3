Originated lines for the window

One originated number per game: a spread, a total and a fair moneyline. These are this repository's numbers. They are not bets, and they are not the market's line.

The market anchor is blank on purpose. The brief asks for the model line to be blended with the closing line, weighted by measured out-of-sample log-loss. There is no licensed closing-line feed here, so there is no second log-loss to measure. The weight is left null rather than invented, and the formula is printed on the method page so the field can be filled the moment a feed exists.

The line is home-team perspective. Fair moneylines are the American price implied by the blended probability on the measured margin spread; they are fair prices, not offers.

How the price is built

Probability
Φ(blend / σ) with σ the measured standard deviation of final home margins (18.3 pts on the captured log).
American odds
−100·p/(1−p) for p < 0.5, else 100·(1−p)/p.
Clamp
probabilities are clamped to [0.02, 0.98]. A model that says a team is a 99.9% favourite on a 22-game log is not being informative.
