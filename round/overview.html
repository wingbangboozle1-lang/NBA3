What this is, and what it refuses to do

NBA3 originates its own NBA lines from official data, then tries to prove the lines are worth anything. This page is the state of that work on 2026-11-16: what was verified, what the model says, and what is still missing.

The headline

Zero strategies are funded. That is not a bug. The rule this project adopted is that a strategy may not lean on a team whose record sits outside a ±2 z-score band, and at 22 captured finals no team has the four games needed for its z-score to mean anything. So every one of the 25 candidate rules is blocked, with the reason printed on each row of the strategy board.

How to read the projection pages

Disagreement
|A − B| in points between the two estimators. It is the primary confidence signal and it is the first number on every line row.
Blend
the mean of the two, published underneath the disagreement, never as the headline.
Total
possessions × points-per-possession, with the rejected alternative (the sum of the two teams' scoring averages) printed beside it.
—
a value that could not be confirmed from an official document. Never a zero, never an average of other values.

What is missing, and what it blocks

Four gaps drive almost every other limitation. Each is on the open questions page with what is known, what is missing, and how to get it.

  • Game-log depth. 22 finals is enough to fit a rating and not enough to prove one. It blocks every luck-based gate and every strategy.
  • A licensed odds feed. Without a closing line there is no market-anchored prior and no way to measure log-loss against a market. The field is left null.
  • Per-player game logs. Without them there is no measured rho for player-prop combos, so those parlay prices are withheld rather than invented.
  • Licensing and jurisdiction. The league's terms restrict gambling use of its statistics, and the operator's jurisdiction is unknown. This is a research dashboard, not a betting service.
Every published value carries an https link to the official NBA document it came from. Anything that could not be confirmed is printed as — and listed on the Flagged for review page. Built by scripts/build_round_site.py; the CI replay fails if this tree drifts from a rebuild.
