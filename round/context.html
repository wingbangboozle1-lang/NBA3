Rest, back-to-back, 3-in-4 and travel

Rest is arithmetic on the official schedule — no estimation. Travel needs a coordinate the NBA does not publish, so it is labelled as a secondary source and every distance names the arena article it used.

Travel is a secondary source, and a lower bound. The coordinates come from each arena's Wikipedia article, not from an NBA page. A great-circle distance between two buildings is a lower bound on travel, not a route. If a coordinate is missing the cell reads — and the row is listed on flagged.
