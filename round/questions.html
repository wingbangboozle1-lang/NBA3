Q11Are the two estimators independent enough for their disagreement to mean anything?They share no input: A reads final margins only, B reads the official advanced page only. The pinned home-court term is common to both, but it is a measured league constant rather than a fitted parameter.A measured correlation between A's error and B's error across a large sample. Today the site claims only that the inputs are disjoint, which is verifiable from the code, not that the errors are uncorrelated, which is not yet measured.www.nba.com

How this list is maintained

The register is data/rounds/2026-11-16/questions.csv. It is read by the build and rendered verbatim; adding a question means adding a row with the five fields above, and the CI replay will fail if the page drifts from the file.

Every published value carries an https link to the official NBA document it came from. Anything that could not be confirmed is printed as — and listed on the Flagged for review page. Built by scripts/build_round_site.py; the CI replay fails if this tree drifts from a rebuild.
