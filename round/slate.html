What is not on this page, and why

  • The market line. No licensed closing-line feed is connected. The model's number is published alone rather than next to an invented market price.
  • A single "official" line. There is no such thing until the two estimators are reconciled; that is what the disagreement column is for.
  • Rest and travel for a team whose previous game is outside the captured window. Those cells read — and are counted on flagged.
