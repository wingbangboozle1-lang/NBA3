Two independent engines in this repository, compared

The repository holds two separately built origination engines. This dashboard's line table uses the walk-forward validated blend (LM-C) from the origin engine. The second engine (the Edge Lab model) publishes its own lines and fair prices for the games it covers. Comparing them on the 17 games they share: 0 of them disagree on which team is favoured, the median spread gap is 3.1 points and the largest is 11.8 points; 1 of the 17 gaps exceed ten points. Neither engine has passed its validation gates, so this table is a cross-check on the lines, not a calibration of either engine.

Read the two spread columns on the same scale. The engines publish a spread field with the same name but opposite sign conventions: the origin engine stores a sportsbook-style line (negative = home favoured) and the Edge Lab engine stores a projected home margin (positive = home favoured). Both columns above are shown in their published form, and the favourite, the gap and the agreement column are all computed after normalising both to one home-margin scale. Comparing the two columns by eye without that step would read a large spurious disagreement. This is recorded as F-24.
