What this is

One dashboard for the whole NBA3 project. It consolidates the eight site trees that earlier sessions produced, re-verifies the underlying numbers against official sources, and keeps a single list of what is confirmed, what is not, and what needs an owner decision. Season 2026-27; results verified through 2026-11-12; slate 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-16 (31 games, three-day maximum).

Read this first. No strategy in this project passes every validation gate, and no price feed is connected, so nothing here is a validated recommendation. The numbers are research output: originated lines, measured correlations, and the evidence that separates skill from luck.
  • F-01 — No licensed odds or price feed exists in this repository (source S18)
  • F-02 — NBA Terms of Use section 9 restricts gambling use of NBA Statistics and data (source S10)
  • F-03 — Dutch rules for offering online sports betting (source S11)

What is not verified

  • No licensed odds or price feed: every line is originated by this project, so no expected value, market edge or de-vigged market probability exists. F-01
  • Player-level correlations, player-prop lines and the points impact of an injury: the underlying data was never captured. Correlations, Player props
  • Availability for the 17 games on 11/15 and 11/16: no injury report has been published for them yet. Injuries
  • Whether the repository's two independent origination engines are telling the same story: they publish the same spread field with opposite sign conventions, so any cross-engine number must be normalised first. Originated lines, F-24
  • Whether NBA data may lawfully be used for wagering purposes. F-02, F-03
