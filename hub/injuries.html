How injury effects are modelled, and what is not modelled

  • Availability is read from the official report; the report has no minutes column, so minutes have to come from elsewhere.
  • Minutes at risk is a count of minutes, not a points estimate. No replacement-player model and no points-per-minute elasticity exist in this repository, so no points impact is published.
  • Players outside the captured top-31 minutes set have a blank impact and an explicit UNVERIFIED note rather than an estimate. See F-08.
