How to read the minutes column

Minutes at risk is the season minutes per game of the players who are out or doubtful, counted only where that player's minutes are in the captured official set. Where the team had not submitted a report, or the player is outside the captured set, the number is 0 or blank and is marked unverified. No points impact is estimated anywhere.
