How the correlations are used

  • Same-game parlay of our own spread and our own total. Residual correlation r = +0.091 with an interval that contains zero, over 114 out-of-sample games. The joint hit rate is 0.246 against 0.219 under independence, so pricing the two legs as independent is roughly right, but only for these legs.
  • Two legs in different games. Independence is assumed, not measured. The row stays unverified.
  • Player props in the same game. Not measurable with the data in this repository; the player-prop parlay question cannot be answered yet. See F-05 and question Q-07.
